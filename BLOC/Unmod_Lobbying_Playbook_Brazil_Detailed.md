## FIRST 10 MINUTES — BEFORE FIRST UNMOD

1. **After your opening GSL, send 3 chits IMMEDIATELY (not after unmod starts):**
   - To Chair: "Brazil — 42-state rolling text statement + IEEE 7007 annex briefing available"
   - To Austria/Chile/Mexico/Ireland/Norway/Switzerland: "Co-drafters — two-tier + reviews — merge drafts? — Brazil (table 3)"
   - To Germany/France/Netherlands: "Two-tier: your regulation draft + our prohibition list = 1 text? — Brazil"

2. **Physical move:** Stand, go to Austria/Ireland cluster first (your bloc core), then Germany/France. Do NOT go to USA/Russia first — you legitimize blockers.

## DURING UNMOD — HOW TO LEAD WITHOUT SAYING "I'M LEADER"

**Brazil's unique tactic: Be the scribe.** Carry a laptop/paper with blank operative clauses OP1-OP6 filled in your position paper. Say: "I’ve typed our two-tier + IEEE annex from the rolling text — who wants to co-edit OP3 verification?" The scribe controls language.

**Script lines (memorize 5):**

1. **Opening a huddle:** "We all voted for 166 — let’s put that into OP1: Protocol VI with Tier 1 bans. Germany, does your regulation wording fit OP2?"
2. **Pulling fence:** "France — you want regulation not ban. Tier 1 only bans human targeting + unpredictable. Your systems stay in Tier 2 under human control. Acceptable?"
3. **Isolating blocker:** "USA says IHL enough but Directive 3000.09 requires human judgment — let’s codify it in OP4 Article 36 reviews. That helps US too — reduces legal risk."
4. **Global South pull:** "Nigeria/South Africa — proliferation to NSAGs is in OP5 transfer controls + assistance clause. Co-sponsor? This is your security clause."
5. **Closing merger:** "We have 3 papers saying same thing. I merged them into one text with 42-state language. Objections to specific words, or can we sponsor together?"

**Who to approach in order:**

1. **Tier 0 (must have):** Austria, Chile, Ireland, Norway, Switzerland — they co-signed with Brazil; merger is easy.
2. **Tier 1 (win bloc):** Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Croatia, Denmark, Finland — offer them OP2 co-draft credit.
3. **Tier 2 (numbers):** Nigeria, South Africa, Sierra Leone, Philippines, Colombia, Peru, Ghana, Kenya — offer OP5 proliferation language.
4. **Tier 3 (swing):** Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia — offer IEEE 7007 technical annex credit.
5. **Avoid early:** USA, Russia, Israel, Belarus — talk to them only after you have 20+ sponsors; then offer them face-saving regulation tier.

## WHAT TO NEVER COMPROMISE vs. WHAT TO TRADE

**Never:** Autonomous human targeting prohibition; MHC = ability to abort; that IHL alone insufficient.

**Can trade:** 
- Whether IEEE annex is binding year 1 or recommendatory with 1-year upgrade
- Logging retention (6 months vs 12 months)
- Geographic scope wording (operational area vs theatre)
- Whether mandate is CCW Protocol VI now or OEWG if CCW fails (keep both tracks)
- Number of capacity-building paragraphs for Global South (give them more — cheap win)

## CHIT WARFARE — INDIAN MUN SPECIFIC

- Chits are scored. Send **every 15 min**, short, with **fact + ask**.
- Bad chit: "We should work together" (0 points)
- Good chit: "Germany — OP2 space-time limits: Brazil proposed 2hr/2km default with override requiring human re-authorization. Agree? — Brazil" (shows drafting, chair notices)
- Direct a chit to chair after each mod you lead: "Brazil notes 2 sponsors added — 18 total — text attached for dais review"

## COMMON TRAPS

- **Don’t fight USA head-on in unmod.** Say: "We need your verification expertise for OP3 — Tier 2 needs US input." Flatter into participation, then bind them.
- **Don’t let chair see you idle.** Always writing, always huddling. If alone, write chits.
- **Don’t promise what breaks Tier 1.** If asked to remove human-targeting ban to get USA sign, say: "We can put that in brackets and vote — but Brazil cannot sponsor text without it. Can we add footnote instead?"

## ENDGAME — LAST 2 HOURS

- Have **sponsors (8-12) + signatories (20+)**. Sponsors = defend text; signatories = want to see it pass.
- Volunteer to read OPs in reading time — confirms you're author.
- In voting, watch for **friendly amendment** offers from USA/UK to weaken Tier 1 — whip your bloc to vote NO on weakening, YES on entire text.
