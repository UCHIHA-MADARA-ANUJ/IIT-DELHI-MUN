# The GSL workflow

**01. RESEARCH**

Know the current facts.

↓

**02. POSITION**

Know what Brazil wants to say.

↓

**03. APPROACH**

Decide what the speech is trying to achieve.

↓

**04. TARGET**

Decide who should react to that argument.

↓

**05. SPEECH**

Write the actual 20 / 45 / 60 / 90-second version.

↓

**06. LOBBY**

Immediately use the speech to start a conversation.

↓

**07. DRAFT**

Turn agreement into a clause.

**Whole room**

### Early MODs
**Build common ground**

Focus:

- prohibition vs regulation;

- predictability.

### Middle MODs
**Make the architecture concrete**

Focus:
- operating envelope;
- lifecycle;
- material-change review;
- accountability.

### Later MODs
**Move toward drafting and institutional questions**

Focus:
- verification;
- capacity-building;
- legal form;
- CCW pathway.

# Targeting philosophy

“Targeting” means:
> **choosing who should hear which argument and who we should approach afterward.**

It does not mean attacking countries.

The objective is:

**speech → reaction → conversation → clause → drafting partner**
