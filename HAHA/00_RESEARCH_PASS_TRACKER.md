# HAHA — RESEARCH PASS TRACKER

This is the bridge between the full `RESEARCH/` library and the practical `HAHA/` command-center files.

## Rule

We do **not** skip the research folder.

The `RESEARCH/` folder is the source base. The `HAHA/` folder is the distilled committee-prep layer.

For every major HAHA file, we first go through the relevant research and then extract:
- what is verified;
- what is useful in committee;
- what needs careful wording;
- what is outdated / superseded;
- what is strategic MUN analysis rather than a documented state position.
