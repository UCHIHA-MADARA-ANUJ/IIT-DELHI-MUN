## MOD CAUCUS MOTIONS YOU SHOULD PROPOSE (IN ORDER OF PRIORITY)

### MOTION 1: Two-Tier Framework (YOUR SIGNATURE - PROPOSE ON DAY 1 MORNING)
**Wording to say:** "Motion to open a moderated caucus on the topic 'Establishing a two-tier prohibitions and regulations framework for LAWS' for a total time of 10 minutes with individual speaker time of 45 seconds."

**Why it benefits Brazil:**
- This IS Brazil's position. By raising it, you frame debate on YOUR terms. Everyone must respond to your tiers.
- Pulls Group B (France, Germany, Japan) into your framework, isolates Group C (USA/Russia) who must argue against both prohibitions AND regulations - harder.
- Chair notes you set agenda - classic Best Delegate move if speech is strong.

**Speech (45 sec):**
> "Brazil proposes the two-tier endorsed by 166 states: tier one prohibits systems that autonomously target humans or whose effects cannot be understood, predicted or limited; tier two regulates all others with mandatory human approval, space-time limits, and verifiable deactivation. This is the rolling text 42 states called ready. It lets us ban the unacceptable while enabling responsible innovation under human control. We invite Germany, France and India to shape the regulation tier with us."

**Points to push in caucus:**
- Get 2-3 states to say "two-tier" on record
- Ask Germany/France: "Would you accept prohibition on human targeting if regulation tier is strong?"

## UNMOD / LOBBYING PLAYBOOK - WHERE BEST DELEGATE IS DECIDED (INDIAN MUN)

### FIRST 30 MINUTES (CRITICAL)

1. **After first speech, send 2 chits:**
   - To Chair: "Brazil notes 166 votes for 79/62 and 42-state rolling text statement (March 2026) - available to brief" - shows research without speaking
   - To Austria/Chile/Mexico: "Austria - Brazil - two-tier + IEEE - draft together? - Brazil" (short, hand-shake chit)
   - To Ireland/Norway/Switzerland: "Co-drafters on rolling text - align clauses? - Brazil"

2. **Stand up first in first unmod.** Do not wait near your seat. Walk to:
   - Priority 1: **Austria** (if present), **Chile, Mexico** - core group. Say: "Hey Austria - we both led ban call in 2018 - want to merge two-tier language? I have IEEE annex draft."
   - Priority 2: **Ireland/Norway/Switzerland** - "We co-signed March statement - let's lock rolling text as basis, I have operative language."
   - Priority 3: **Germany/France** - "I know you want regulation tier - Brazil's tier two gives you that with space-time limits - can we co-sponsor?"

**Exact opening lines (memorize):**

*To Austria/Chile:*
> "Hi - Brazil here. We both called for binding instrument in 2018, and we delivered the 42-state statement together. I think if we combine your prohibition language with Brazil's IEEE standard annex, we can pull Germany and France too. Want to draft operative 1 and 2 together? I can scribe."

*To France/Germany (fence-sitters):*
> "Hey France - I heard your mod speech wanting regulation not just ban - Brazil's two-tier does exactly that. We ban only unpredictable/human-targeting, we regulate rest with human control. Would you be comfortable co-sponsoring if we add your verification idea?"

*To South Africa/Nigeria/Philippines (NAM):*
> "Hi - Brazil's pushing pre-emptive regulation before arms race locks Global South out - plus tech transfer fund for verification. That's NAM priority too, right? Can we count you as signatory?"

*To India (if strong delegate):*
> "India - I know we both care about strategic autonomy - Brazil's proposal protects your development under human control, not denies it. IEEE standard even helps with Article 36 reviews. Want to compare drafts before we go to larger group?"

**What NOT to say:**
- "Join my bloc" (arrogant)
- "I am the bloc leader" (chair will mark you as dominating)
- "We must ban everything" (scares Group B)

**How to become bloc leader without looking arrogant:**
- Be the scribe: "Can I write down what we agree so we don't lose it? I'll share doc."
- Be the merger: "Germany's idea on space limits is better than mine - let's use theirs, I'll add IEEE citation."
- Be the presenter: Volunteer to present bloc's position to chair when chair asks - but say "On behalf of Austria, Chile, Brazil and Germany..."

### WHAT YOU WANT IN RESOLUTION (YOUR 3 CLAUSES - FIGHT FOR THESE)

**Clause 1 (Prohibitions - YOUR RED LINE):**
> *Prohibits development, production, transfer and use of AWS that autonomously select and engage human targets, or whose effects cannot be understood, predicted, explained, directed at a military objective, or limited as required by IHL.*

**Clause 2 (Regulations):**
> *Requires meaningful human control over critical functions of all other AWS, including: (a) human approval of each engagement with sufficient situational awareness; (b) strict geographical and temporal limits; (c) ability to supervise, intervene, and deactivate/self-destruct; (d) prohibition on changing mission parameters without human validation.*

**Clause 3 (Verifiability - YOUR SIGNATURE):**
> *Establishes technical annex based on IEEE Standard 7007-2021 and mandatory Article 36 legal reviews, requiring states to certify, log engagement decisions, and cooperate on verification, with assistance for developing states.*

**Clause 4 (Process - MANDATE):**
> *Recommends the Seventh CCW Review Conference (2026) authorize negotiations on legally binding protocol based on GGE rolling text, complemented by UNGA informal consultations, aiming to conclude by end-2026 per UNSG/ICRC call, with moratorium on prohibited systems pending entry into force.*

**What to compromise:** Wording of moratorium (maybe "calls upon" not "decides"), timeline (2027 if needed), funding mechanism details.

**What to NEVER compromise:** Prohibition on human targeting, MHC over critical functions, legally binding form.
