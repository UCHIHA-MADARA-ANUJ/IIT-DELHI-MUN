# POINT OF ORDER — DISEC LAWS | Indian MUN Guide

This folder = **How to use Points of Order strategically** (and not annoy chair).

## INDIAN MUN REALITY:

**Valid Point of Order (use):**
- Factual error: "Point of order - delegate stated Brazil abstained on 78/241, Brazil voted FOR and co-sponsored"
- Procedure: "Point of order - speaker time has exceeded 90 seconds" (use sparingly, only vs blockers who ramble)
- Personal privilege: "Point of personal privilege - could delegate speak louder, inaudible"

**INVALID (will get you cut off and look like novice):**
- "Point of order - delegate is lying" (say "factual inaccuracy" not "lying")
- "Point of order - delegate's argument is illogical" (that's POI, not POO)
- Raising POO to make a speech

## HOW BEST DELEGATES USE POO:

1.  **Fact correction with citation:** 
    > "Point of order — factual correction: Brazil did not abstain on Resolution 79/62; Brazil co-sponsored and voted FOR with 165 others on Dec 2, 2024. The voting record is 166-3-15."

2.  **Procedure to regain floor:**
    > "Point of order — the delegate has exceeded time and is introducing new substantive argument outside MOD scope — request chair to direct back to human control."

3.  **Don't overuse:** 1-2 POOs in 2 days max. More makes you look desperate. Use POIs for argument, POO only for clear error.

**BRAZIL-SPECIFIC POO OPPORTUNITIES:**
- When opponent says "Brazil makes Falcão killer robots" → POO: "Falcão is reconnaissance, human-in-the-loop, under Article 36 review — not autonomous targeting — factual correction"
- When opponent says "No consensus for treaty" → POO: "166 vs 3 is consensus minus 3, not lack of consensus"

## PHRASE TO USE:
> "Point of order, honourable chair — [factual/procedural correction] — thank you."

Short, polite, cited. Chair remembers you as precise.
