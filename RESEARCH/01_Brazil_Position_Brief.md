## 1. BRAZIL'S OFFICIAL POSITION (VERIFIED)

**One-line stance:** Brazil **supports a legally binding instrument with prohibitions and regulations (two-tier approach) to ensure meaningful human control over LAWS**, preferably as a new Protocol VI to the CCW, but is willing to explore any forum (including UNGA) if CCW consensus blocks progress. [1](https://automatedresearch.org/news/state_position/brazil/)

**Evolution:**
- May 2013: At Human Rights Council, Brazil warned LAWS would trivialize war and create accountability gaps.
- Nov 2017: Brazil explicitly called for a **ban on fully autonomous weapons** incompatible with IHL/IHRL. [9](https://www.hrw.org/report/2020/08/10/stopping-killer-robots/country-positions-banning-fully-autonomous-weapons-and)
- Aug 2018: **Austria, Brazil, Chile** jointly proposed mandate to negotiate legally binding instrument ensuring meaningful human control. [9](https://www.hrw.org/report/2020/08/10/stopping-killer-robots/country-positions-banning-fully-autonomous-weapons-and)
- 2021: Brazil (with Chile/Mexico) submitted outline for prohibitions + regulations: ban AWS that cannot be controlled, cannot distinguish, cause superfluous injury; require human command chain, space/time limits, ability to intervene/deactivate. [10](https://documents.unoda.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Brazil-Chile-Mexico.pdf)
- 2024-2026: Brazil became **bloc leader** for regulation. In Sept 2025, Brazil delivered **joint statement on behalf of 42 states** (cross-regional) saying rolling text is sufficient basis to start negotiations and they are "ready to move ahead". [4](https://www.stopkillerrobots.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SKR_CCW-paper-March2026_FINAL.pdf) Repeated in March 2026 GGE: Brazil said 42 states now support rolling text, and GGE should be in position by Sept 2026 (your MUN month) to submit text to Review Conference for negotiation mandate. [1](https://automatedresearch.org/news/state_position/brazil/)
- Brazil **co-sponsored and voted FOR** every UNGA LAWS resolution: 78/241 (Dec 2023 - 152 votes), 79/62 / L.77 (Dec 2, 2024 - 166 in favor, 3 against: Belarus, DPRK, Russia, 15 abstentions) [2](https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/05/21/un-start-talks-treaty-ban-killer-robots), and 80/57 (Dec 2025 - calls to complete elements for instrument). [1](https://automatedresearch.org/news/state_position/brazil/)
- Brazil endorsed Vienna Conference Chair's Summary (2024) committing to work with urgency for international legal instrument. [1](https://automatedresearch.org/news/state_position/brazil/)

### What "two-tier" means for Brazil (from its UNSG submission 2024):
**Tier 1 - PROHIBITIONS:** Ban AWS that:
- Cannot be meaningfully controlled by humans
- Cannot be understood/predicted/explained by user
- Cannot be directed at specific military objective (violates distinction)
- May cause disproportionate harm / superfluous injury
- Target humans directly or operate without human validation [3](https://docs-library.unoda.org/General_Assembly_First_Committee_-Seventy-Ninth_session_(2024)/78-241-Brazil-EN.pdf)

**Tier 2 - REGULATIONS:** Positive obligations for all other AWS:
- Human operator must retain ultimate decision on critical functions (target selection + engagement)
- Impose space/time limits, avoid civilian concentrations
- Require situational awareness before approving force
- Ability to supervise, intervene, deactivate/self-destruct
- Precautions to prevent changing mission parameters without human validation
- Mandatory Article 36 legal reviews [3](https://docs-library.unoda.org/General_Assembly_First_Committee_-Seventy-Ninth_session_(2024)/78-241-Brazil-EN.pdf)

### Key Definitions Brazil Uses:
- **ICRC definition:** "AWS = weapon system designed to select and engage one or more targets without need for human intervention after activation." Brazil supports this. [3](https://docs-library.unoda.org/General_Assembly_First_Committee_-Seventy-Ninth_session_(2024)/78-241-Brazil-EN.pdf)
- **Meaningful Human Control (MHC):** Not just a button press. Requires understanding, prediction, explanation, and ability to intervene. Brazil argues MHC is **implicitly required by IHL** (distinction, proportionality, military necessity) and IHRL. [2](https://automatedresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Overview-of-Submissions-UNSG-report-on-autonomous-weapons-FINAL-.pdf)
- **Technical standard:** Brazil tabled WP.1 (2024) promoting **IEEE STD 7007-2021** (first global ontological standard for ethically driven robotics) as common platform for control mechanisms, limits on targets/duration/geography. [3](https://docs-library.unoda.org/General_Assembly_First_Committee_-Seventy-Ninth_session_(2024)/78-241-Brazil-EN.pdf)

## 3. TREATIES, LAW & VOTING HISTORY YOU CAN QUOTE

**Brazil is party to:**
- CCW + All Protocols (including Protocol V on Explosive Remnants of War) - but NOT Oslo Cluster Munitions Convention
- Geneva Conventions + Additional Protocol I (Art. 36 legal review obligation)
- Ottawa Convention (Anti-Personnel Mines) - banned
- Treaty of Tlatelolco (Latin America nuclear-weapon-free), NPT, Arms Trade Treaty (ATT)
- Voted FOR UNGA 78/241 (2023), 79/62 (2024), 80/57 (2025)

**Law to cite in speeches:**
- **Martens Clause:** Civilians/combatants remain under protection of principles of humanity + dictates of public conscience - Brazil says LAWS without MHC violate this.
- **Article 36, Additional Protocol I:** Obligation to review new weapons for IHL compliance. Brazil says this is insufficient alone - need specific LAWS instrument.
- **IHL Principles:** Distinction, Proportionality, Precaution, Military Necessity - Brazil argues autonomous systems cannot be programmed to apply these in uncertain conditions. [3](https://docs-library.unoda.org/General_Assembly_First_Committee_-Seventy-Ninth_session_(2024)/78-241-Brazil-EN.pdf)
- **ICJ Advisory Opinion:** "IHL applies to all forms of warfare and all kinds of weapons, those of the past, present and future." So LAWS are not in legal vacuum.

**Voting history specifics:**
- Dec 2, 2024 - UNGA First Committee L.77 / GA Res 79/62: **166 FOR**, 3 AGAINST (Belarus, DPRK, Russia), 15 ABSTENTIONS (incl. China, India, Iran, Israel, Turkey). Brazil co-sponsored, voted FOR. [2](https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/05/21/un-start-talks-treaty-ban-killer-robots)
- Dec 1, 2025 - GA adopted L.41: **164 FOR**, 6 AGAINST (Belarus, Burundi, DPRK, Israel, Russia, USA), 7 ABSTENTIONS (Argentina, China...). Brazil co-sponsored, voted FOR. [1](https://press.un.org/en/2025/ga12736.doc.htm)
- 2025 NY Informal Consultations (mandated by 79/62): May 2025, 2 days in New York - Brazil delivered joint statement of 42.

- **China - THE SWING:** Abstained on both 2024 and 2025 resolutions. ## 6. BRAZIL'S WEAKNESSES - WHAT WILL GET YOU ATTACKED

**You MUST prepare for these. Chairs love when Brazil gets hit on hypocrisy.**

1. **Cluster Munitions Hypocrisy**
   - Fact: Brazil **never used cluster munitions** but **refuses to join Oslo Convention on Cluster Munitions**, arguing CCW is better forum and Oslo has loopholes. Voted abstention on some cluster ban resolutions. [1](https://academic.oup.com/fpa/article/21/3/oraf019/8136451)
   - Attack line: "Brazil claims to be disarmament champion but cherry-picks - banned landmines when cheap, but protects cluster munitions because Avibras makes them. Now you want to ban LAWS you don't even have - easy to be moral when you have no stake."
   - Your defense: "Brazil is party to ALL CCW Protocols including Protocol V on explosive remnants, regulates use under IHL, and position was about strengthening CCW inclusivity, not protecting industry. Our 2024 UNSG submission shows we support prohibition where systems violate IHL - consistency, not hypocrisy. We apply same CCW-first logic to LAWS but have learned delay costs - that's why we now support rolling text negotiations."

2. **Defense Industry Ties - Embraer / Avibras / Elbit (Israel)**
   - Fact: Harpia Systems = Embraer (51%) + AEL (Elbit Systems subsidiary, 40%) + Avibras (9%) developing **Falcão UAV** for Brazilian Air Force/Army (SISFRON border surveillance, $4bn program). [1](https://www.uasvision.com/2013/02/07/brazil-avibras-embraer-elbit-harpia-uas/) Though surveillance-focused, it's dual-use and partnership with Israeli Elbit - which supplies LAWS-related tech to Gaza.
   - Attack: "Brazil partners with Elbit - a supplier of autonomous weapons used in Gaza - while preaching ban. And SISFRON drones could be armed autonomously. Is Brazil developing what it wants to ban?"
   - Defense: "Falcão is reconnaissance/target acquisition/surveillance - human-in-the-loop systems for border monitoring, fully under human control, subject to Article 36 reviews. Brazil's call for IEEE 7007 standards and legal reviews proves we hold even our own systems to highest standard. Partnership is for sovereignty over Amazon, not autonomy in targeting. We invite international verification."

3. **BRICS Contradiction**
   - Fact: Brazil is BRICS with Russia/China who OPPOSE LAWS treaty. Brazil says "non-intervention / autonomy."
   - Attack: "How can Brazil claim to lead Global South on LAWS while in BRICS with Russia who blocks every negotiation? Is Brazil serious or just posturing for UNSC seat?"
   - Defense: "Exactly proves our autonomy - Article 4 of our Constitution requires independent foreign policy. We co-sign with Ireland/Norway/Switzerland AND Latin America, not with BRICS on this issue. We voted FOR resolutions Russia voted AGAINST - 166 vs 3. That's not posturing, that's principle over bloc."

4. **No LAWS Capability = Cheap Talk?**
   - Attack: "Brazil has no advanced LAWS, so banning costs you nothing - like your landmine ban. Easy moralism without sacrifice. Tech powers bear burden."
   - Defense: "Prevention is cheaper than proliferation. Brazil and 62% of its people know Amazon surveillance tech could evolve to LAWS - we regulate BEFORE deployment, not after. That's leadership, per Guterres' 2026 deadline. And our IEEE standard proposal shows we contribute technically, not just morally."

5. **Amazon & Domestic Human Rights**
   - Stretch attack: "Brazil uses surveillance drones over Amazon indigenous lands - human rights concerns. Who are you to lecture on human dignity?"
   - Defense: "Surveillance with human control and judicial oversight is IHL/IHRL compliant. LAWS removing human judgment is categorically different. We support human rights law applying to ALL systems."

6. **Process Flip-Flop**
   - Attack: "Brazil said CCW is central for cluster munitions, now wants to bypass CCW for LAWS via UNGA - forum shopping."
   - Defense: "Brazil still prefers CCW Protocol - our March 2026 statement says rolling text under CCW is basis. But inclusivity cannot be excuse for indefinite delay. We support CCW AND UNGA informal consultations as complementary - Brazil voted FOR both tracks, as stated in Explanation of Vote 2023."
