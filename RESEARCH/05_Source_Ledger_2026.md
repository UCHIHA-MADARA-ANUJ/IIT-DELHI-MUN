### Treaty / legal texts
- Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons: UN Treaty Collection / CCW official materials.
- Additional Protocol I, Article 36: verify from ICRC / official treaty text before quoting.
- Rome Statute, Arms Trade Treaty, Ottawa Convention and Tlatelolco: use official treaty repositories/UN Treaty Collection.

## Tier 2 — High-quality secondary sources

- ICRC — autonomous weapons and IHL analysis.
- UNIDIR — LAWS and IHL / military AI research.
- SIPRI — responsible military AI and procurement research.
- Reaching Critical Will — CCW statements and procedural reporting.
- Human Rights Watch / Campaign to Stop Killer Robots — useful country-position compilations, but verify their claims against primary statements before using them as evidence of a state's official position.

## Current research standards

1. Never quote a vote from a secondary article if the UN voting record is available.
2. Never say a country "supports a ban" based only on a general humanitarian statement; find the exact wording.
3. Separate **legal fact**, **state position**, **analysis**, and **advocacy claim**.
4. Preserve publication date and session/year for every current-position claim.
5. For 2026 claims, prioritize documents from the 2026 GGE sessions and official state statements.
6. When sources disagree, record the disagreement rather than silently selecting one.
