## Research standard

Every exact-matrix dossier is being normalized to cover:
- official LAWS position;
- 2023/2024/2025 UNGA votes;
- 2025 sponsorship;
- CCW status and GGE participation;
- 2026 position;
- legal form;
- prohibitions/regulations;
- human control;
- Article 36/weapons review;
- verification/lifecycle;
- transfer/proliferation;
- negotiating strengths;
- documented constraints;
- Brazil-specific overlap and unanswered questions.

## Important distinction

“Existing dossier” does **not** mean “research complete.” Many older files were produced at different times and with different evidence standards. They are being audited against the new 2026 source hierarchy.

## No premature bloc

This index deliberately does not classify countries as “best allies”, “targets”, “enemies” or “bloc members”. Any eventual bloc structure must come from the evidence plus live lobbying conditions.
