
# 16 — Deep Research Checklist

Before moving from agenda research to bloc architecture, the following should be fully covered.

## Law
- distinction
- proportionality
- precautions
- indiscriminate attacks
- unnecessary suffering/superfluous injury
- Article 36
- command responsibility
- State responsibility
- Martens Clause
- interaction with international human rights law
- relationship to existing CCW Protocols

## Technology
- autonomy spectrum
- critical targeting functions
- sensor and classification errors
- adaptive systems
- machine learning uncertainty
- adversarial manipulation
- cybersecurity
- communications loss
- swarms
- AI decision support
- software/model updates

## Operational use
- defensive systems
- offensive systems
- target categories
- urban environments
- open environments
- duration
- geographic boundaries
- engagement scale
- human intervention

## Governance
- definitions
- prohibited categories
- regulated categories
- lifecycle controls
- testing/evaluation
- legal review
- procurement
- training
- auditability
- traceability
- incident reporting
- verification
- transfers
- non-State actors
- capacity-building

## International process
- 2019 Guiding Principles
- 2021–2024 GGE development
- 2025 rolling-text work
- 2025 42-HCP working paper
- March 2026 GGE
- August–September 2026 GGE
- 4 September 2026 consensus final report
- 7–9 September 2026 PrepCom
- 16–20 November 2026 Seventh Review Conference

## State comparison
At minimum, compare every MUN State on:
- scope;
- legal form;
- prohibitions;
- human control;
- lifecycle;
- testing;
- verification;
- transfers;
- capacity-building.

## Evidence hygiene
For each major fact:
- identify source;
- identify date;
- identify whether it is law, negotiation text, State position, expert analysis or inference;
- avoid treating a vote for a resolution as agreement with every substantive clause;
- mark outdated positions as historical;
- do not use unverified battlefield claims as settled facts.

## Completion condition

Agenda research is "ready for bloc work" only when the team can answer:
**"What are the actual policy components available to negotiate, where is international convergence, and what disagreements are substantive rather than merely terminological?"**
