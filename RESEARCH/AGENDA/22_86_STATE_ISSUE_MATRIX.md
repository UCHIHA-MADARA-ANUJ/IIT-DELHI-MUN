
# 22 — 86-State Issue Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix converts the country work into the variables that will later determine bloc compatibility.

**Important:** this is a descriptive research matrix, not a ranking or alliance list.

### Evidence codes

- **B** — explicit binding-instrument signal in the current/monitored record.
- **R** — explicit support for the rolling-text / GGE negotiating pathway, without assuming unconditional treaty support.
- **C** — cautious/conditional approach, or support for regulation without verified commitment to Brazil's preferred legal form.
- **U** — unresolved in the current evidence set; do not infer.
- **N** — explicitly opposed/negative signal in the current record.

For technical dimensions:
- **H** — explicit human-control/judgement emphasis.
- **2T** — two-tier/prohibit-some + regulate-others approach explicitly identified.
- **LC** — lifecycle/testing/accountability emphasis.
- **A** — exact provision requires primary-source audit.

## Reading the matrix

Three categories should remain separate:

### Confirmed convergence
A country has an explicit current or recent primary-source position on a specific component.

### Procedural convergence
A country has joined the rolling-text group or otherwise supported continued GGE work. This does not automatically equal agreement with every substantive clause.

### Unresolved
No sufficiently specific current primary source was located. This is a research gap, not evidence of opposition.

## What this means for later bloc work

The matrix gives us the raw material to build:
- a substantive-core group by policy component;
- a second ring of conditional/bridge delegations;
- countries requiring direct clarification during lobbying.

It does not yet select a bloc.
