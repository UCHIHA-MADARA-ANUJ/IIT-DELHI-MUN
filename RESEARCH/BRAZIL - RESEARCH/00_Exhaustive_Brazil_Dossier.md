## 1. ONE-PAGE EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (MEMORIZE THIS)

**Stance:** Brazil **supports a legally binding instrument with two tiers — prohibitions + regulations — ensuring meaningful human control (MHC) over LAWS**, preferably as **new CCW Protocol VI** based on **GGE rolling text**, complemented by UNGA informal consultations. Not NGO maximalism: ban only what can *never* comply (human-targeting, unpredictable, indiscriminate), regulate rest with strict human control.

**Signature moves you MUST cite:**
- **42-state joint statement delivered by Brazil (Sept 2025 + March 2026)** calling rolling text "sufficient basis for negotiation" — cross-regional, one-third of CCW [1](https://www.stopkillerrobots.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SKR_CCW-paper-March2026_FINAL.pdf)

  - **PROSUB:** 4 Scorpène-derived Riachuelo conventional (lead in service 2022) + **Álvaro Alberto SN-BR nuclear-attack submarine** (pressure-hull integration 2025) [17](https://militaryspend.org/country-profiles/brazil).
  - **Tamandaré frigates:** F200 delivered 2025, built ThyssenKrupp/Embraer/Atech [17](https://militaryspend.org/country-profiles/brazil).
  - **KC-390 Millennium, Guaraní APC, Astros 2020 MRL** [19](https://grokipedia.com/page/Brazilian_Armed_Forces).
