## 1. CONSTITUTIONAL FOUNDATION (1988 Constitution)

**Art.1, Clause I:** National sovereignty is *fundamental principle* — non-negotiable. Military Penal Code Art.27: treason is very serious crime [1](https://bricsbrasil.com.br/en/sovereignty/).

**Art.4:** International relations governed by [2](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R46236/R46236.10.pdf):
- National independence
- Prevalence of human rights
- Self-determination of peoples
- **Non-intervention**
- Equality among nations
- **Defense of peace**
- **Peaceful settlement of conflicts**
- Repudiation of terrorism and racism
- Cooperation among peoples for progress
- Granting political asylum

**Exact quote from source:** "Article 4 of Brazil's 1988 constitution, as amended, states that Brazil's international relations are governed by the following principles: national independence; prevalence of human rights; self-determination; nonintervention; equality among nations; defense of peace; peaceful settlement of conflicts..." [2](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R46236/R46236.10.pdf)

**Itamaraty doctrine:** 1,500-strong professional foreign service, known for independent foreign policy, refusal to subordinate to great powers [3](https://cebri.org/revista/en/artigo/202/brazil-the-brics-and-active-non-alignment). President has ultimate authority, Congress reviews treaties [Foreign relations of Brazil].

**Your DISEC language:** Always anchor to Art.4. Example: "Per Article 4, Brazil's parallel pursuit of human rights and peaceful settlement requires MHC — algorithmic killing without human control violates both."

## 2. ACTIVE NON-ALIGNMENT (ANA) — Lula 3.0 Doctrine (2023-2026)

**Definition:** Fortin, Heine & Ominami (2020, 2023): Developing nations must put national interest front, refusing to align with Washington *or* Beijing. Weaker powers hedge bets [3](https://cebri.org/revista/en/artigo/202/brazil-the-brics-and-active-non-alignment).

**Lula's articulation:**
- Victory speech 30 Oct 2022: "We will not accept a new Cold War between US and China. We will have relations with everyone." [4](https://jacobin.com/2022/12/lula-brics-economic-sovereignty-multipolar-world-non-aligned-movement)
- With Chile's Boric, April 2025 (re tariffs): "We don't want to trade with the United States *or* with China, we want to trade with *both*" — commitment to autonomy, refusal to choose side [3](https://cebri.org/revista/en/artigo/202/brazil-the-brics-and-active-non-alignment)
- Rio BRICS Summit 6 July 2025: "BRICS is the heir to the Non-Aligned Movement. With multilateralism under attack, our autonomy is in check once again." [5](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/leaders-growing-brics-group-gather-rio-summit-2025-07-06/) Same summit: "If international governance does not reflect 21st century multipolar reality, it is up to BRICS to bring it up to date." [6](https://www.stimson.org/2025/2025-brics-summit-takeaways-and-projections/)

**Hedging in practice:**
- **BRI refusal:** Lula decided **NOT to sign** China's Belt and Road Initiative despite pressure, to avoid rattling US — mixed signals as ANA tactic [3](https://cebri.org/revista/en/artigo/202/brazil-the-brics-and-active-non-alignment). Tactical equivocation.
- **WWII precedent:** ECFR notes Brazil hedged between Nazi Germany and Allies until alignment with Allies became more beneficial — same hedging logic today [7](https://ecfr.eu/publication/brazil-europes-bridge-to-the-global-south/).

**ECFR Assessment (9 Sept 2025):** [7](https://ecfr.eu/publication/brazil-europes-bridge-to-the-global-south/)
- Three leitmotifs: (1) autonomy vs main power centres, (2) search for great power role, (3) qualified commitment to multilateralism.
- "Brazil has long history pursuing independence, counterbalancing US influence while maintaining flexibility — limited gains from aligning with US vs South Korea benefits."
- Under Lula: embraces post-US order ("US to take different role rather than lead"), hedges between US and China capitalizing on competition — opportunities unavailable during US dominance.
- Sees self not as middle power but *grande nação* (great nation) — deserves greater voice, actively addresses global issues (non-proliferation, climate) at UNGA 2023/2024 [7](https://ecfr.eu/publication/brazil-europes-bridge-to-the-global-south/).

**Your attack defense:** When Russia asks "Why with Ireland not BRICS?" — Answer: "Active Non-Alignment — we deliver 42-state statement with Ireland/Norway/Switzerland *and* chair BRICS. That's autonomy, not alignment."

**Brazil's 4th chair:** 2010, 2014, 2019, **2025** [3](https://cebri.org/revista/en/artigo/202/brazil-the-brics-and-active-non-alignment). Chairing G20 (2024), BRICS+ and COP30 (2025) in 13 months — "diplomatic hub" [3](https://cebri.org/revista/en/artigo/202/brazil-the-brics-and-active-non-alignment).

**Rio Summit 2025 (6-7 July):** [5](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/leaders-growing-brics-group-gather-rio-summit-2025-07-06/)[6](https://www.stimson.org/2025/2025-brics-summit-takeaways-and-projections/)

## 4. MULTILATERALISM & UNSC BID

**Stated goal:** Permanent seat on UN Security Council — repeatedly frustrated, perception US/Europe won't cede space — reason for BRICS leverage [2](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R46236/R46236.10.pdf).

**Approach:** Pursue cooperative relations with both US/EU and BRICS (Russia, India, China, South Africa) simultaneously [2](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R46236/R46236.10.pdf). Lula reasserts influence after 8 years of low profile (Bolsonaro era).

**ECFR nuance:** [7](https://ecfr.eu/publication/brazil-europes-bridge-to-the-global-south/)
- Non-nuclear, limited military capacity, secure geography (no external threats) → foreign policy emphasizes diplomacy over force.
- Preference for peaceful settlement — why Brazil mediates (Iran nuclear 2010, Haiti peacekeeping lead, proposed "G20 for peace" for Ukraine, Brazil-China joint peace proposal May 2024) [7](https://ecfr.eu/publication/brazil-europes-bridge-to-the-global-south/).
- But increasingly self-restraining on issues sensitive to China (to keep BRICS financing) — risk of appearing ideologically aligned with China on Ukraine/Gaza, hurting trust with EU.

**Americas Quarterly Feb 2024:** Balancing act — harmonize interests to expand geopolitical/economic influence, diversify business, reduce dependence on Western rules, while convincing that OPEC+ join doesn't contradict green power/COP30 host [11](https://www.americasquarterly.org/article/a-balancing-act-for-brazils-foreign-policy/).

## 6. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR LAWS

- Brazil *must* advocate **multilateral, inclusive, legally binding** — not US voluntary or Russia nil.
- Must show **bridge**: Quote both Global South (62% poll) *and* Europe (Ireland/Norway/Switzerland co-drafters) — not BRICS.
- Must stress **peaceful settlement + human rights** (Art.4) to justify MHC.
- Must avoid looking anti-tech — PBIA $4B proves pro-AI with sovereignty.
