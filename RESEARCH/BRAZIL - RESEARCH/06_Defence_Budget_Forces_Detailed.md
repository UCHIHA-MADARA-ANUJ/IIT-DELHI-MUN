## 3. MAJOR PROGRAMS — WHERE MONEY GOES

### SISFRON — Integrated Border Monitoring System (Your LAWS-relevant program)
- **What:** Border surveillance network: ground radars, sensors, SATCOM, C2, UAVs for Amazon + Paraguayan borders. Complements **SIVAM** (Amazon Surveillance System since early 2000s — primary/secondary radars, remote sensing aircraft, data centres) [2](https://grokipedia.com/page/Brazilian_Armed_Forces).
- **Cost:** **$4B Phase 1** (Embraer, Army) [Haripia docs]; **$2.4B acquisitions 2024-2027** for additional sensors/vehicles to expand coverage [2](https://grokipedia.com/page/Brazilian_Armed_Forces); **R$30B off-budget funds evaluated Sept 2025** to accelerate alongside other modernizations [2](https://grokipedia.com/page/Brazilian_Armed_Forces).
- **Timeline:** By 2023 partial, full operational capability **2035**, prioritizing high-threat arcs (Amazonian/Paraguayan) [2](https://grokipedia.com/page/Brazilian_Armed_Forces).
- **Link:** Phase 2 adds UAV capability [UASVision 2013]. This is *dual-use* — opponent will say "surveillance today, autonomous targeting tomorrow."
- **Your defense:** "SISFRON is human-in-the-loop under Article 36, with SATCOM for human flexibility — Brazil tabled IEEE 7007 to ensure even border drones remain abortable."

### Other capital programs (show you are not disarming):
- **F-X2 Gripen E/F (F-39E/F):** 36 aircraft, Saab/Embraer, delivered via Embraer Gavião Peixoto, driving 2025 spend [4](https://militaryspend.org/country-profiles/brazil).
- **PROSUB:** 4 Riachuelo-class conventional (lead in service 2022) + **Álvaro Alberto SN-BR nuclear-attack submarine** — pressure-hull integration 2025 [4](https://militaryspend.org/country-profiles/brazil).
- **Tamandaré frigates:** F200 Tamandaré delivered 2025, consortium ThyssenKrupp/Embraer/Atech, Itajaí [4](https://militaryspend.org/country-profiles/brazil).
- **KC-390 Millennium, Guaraní armored vehicles, Astros 2020 MRL** [2](https://grokipedia.com/page/Brazilian_Armed_Forces).
- **Air Force ISR study (Apr 2024):** Brazilian Air Force + Embraer joint study to adapt aircraft for intelligence/surveillance/reconnaissance, decision by 2026 — if C-390 MPA favored no tender, else 6 MPAs $3B 2031-2036 [6](https://www.defensenews.com/air/2024/04/12/brazilian-air-force-embraer-launch-study-for-surveillance-fleet/).

**R&D:** Modest vs GDP but capable [4](https://militaryspend.org/country-profiles/brazil). Exports **R$8.96B (USD 1.6B) early 2024** record [2](https://grokipedia.com/page/Brazilian_Armed_Forces).
