## 3. AVIBRAS — "100% Domestic"

Founded 1961, engineering, conceptualizes/develops/manufactures defence & civilian: **artillery rocket systems, autonomous and guided missiles, anti-aircraft, armament systems, armored vehicles, UAS, C2, complex systems integration** [4](https://www.uasvision.com/2013/02/07/brazil-avibras-embraer-elbit-harpia-uas/).

Portfolio explicitly includes **autonomous missiles** — opponent may quote this as "Brazil makes autonomous weapons."

**Your distinction:** "Autonomous" in Avibras portfolio = autonomous *flight* or *navigation* (like cruise missile), not autonomous *target selection without human*. Emphasize human-in-the-loop for targeting.

## 5. HOW TO DEFEND IN COMMITTEE (3 Levels)

**Level 1 — Technical:** Falcão is **reconnaissance/target acquisition/support** — human approval required — not Tier 1 prohibited. Under **Article 36 review**.

**Level 2 — Legal:** Brazil tabled **IEEE 7007** to hold *own* systems to same standard — invite verification. Sovereign control via Law 12.598 ensures *national* ability to enforce human control, not outsourced.

**Level 3 — Strategic:** Embraer success proves Brazil can maintain autonomy *without* choosing China — alternative development model [2](https://defense.info/global-dynamics/2026/03/embraer-a-different-brazilian-story/). Autonomy requires sovereign industry, but sovereign industry requires MHC rules.

**One-liner:** "We built Falcão to watch the Amazon with a pilot's eyes, not to let code decide life and death."
