## 1. EBIA — Brazilian Strategy for Artificial Intelligence (2021)

**Launch:** MCTI Ordinance No.4,617 6 Apr 2021, amended 4,979 13 Jul 2021 — **first federal strategy dedicated exclusively to AI** [1](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/brazilian-ai-strategy-2027)[2](https://www.essex.ac.uk/research-projects/ai-policy-observatory-for-the-world-of-work/national-and-regional-cases/brazil).

**Context:** Part of series: **Brazilian Strategy for Digital Transformation (E-Digital, 2018)** identified AI as central [2](https://www.essex.ac.uk/research-projects/ai-policy-observatory-for-the-world-of-work/national-and-regional-cases/brazil), plus **LGPD (General Data Protection Law)** [1](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/brazilian-ai-strategy-2027).

**Process:** 2 years public consultation, ~1000 contributions, consultancy hired [1](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/brazilian-ai-strategy-2027).

**Objectives (from OECD):** [1](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/brazilian-ai-strategy-2027)
- Contribute to ethical principles for responsible development/use
- Promote sustained investment in R&D
- Remove barriers for innovation
- Enable/educate professionals for AI ecosystem
- Stimulate innovation in international environment
- Promote cooperation among public/private, industry, research centres

**Structure:** Levers: legislation, regulation, ethical use; AI governance; international aspects [2](https://www.essex.ac.uk/research-projects/ai-policy-observatory-for-the-world-of-work/national-and-regional-cases/brazil). **74 strategic actions** [2](https://www.essex.ac.uk/research-projects/ai-policy-observatory-for-the-world-of-work/national-and-regional-cases/brazil).

**Defence & Security Recognition:** EBIA explicitly recognizes **national defence and public security as strategic sectors** that should benefit from AI [3](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/artificial-intelligence-2025/brazil/trends-and-developments).
- Objectives: promote R&D of AI for **national defence**, encourage adoption for **situational awareness, risk analysis, decision support systems** [3](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/artificial-intelligence-2025/brazil/trends-and-developments).
- Early but clear recognition of AI's role in enhancing national security.

**Current uses (as of 2025):** border surveillance, cybersecurity & cyber defence, **autonomous aerial and ground surveillance (high-capacity drones)**, public security operations [3](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/artificial-intelligence-2025/brazil/trends-and-developments).

## 2. PBIA — Brazilian Artificial Intelligence Plan 2024-2028 ("AI for the Good of All")

**Launch:** 30 July 2024, Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MCTI), during 5th National Conference on Science, Technology and Innovation [4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp). Titled **"AI for the Good of All"** [4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp).

**Goals (official):** [1](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/brazilian-ai-strategy-2027)[4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp)
- Improve lives via AI innovations in productive capacity & social well-being
- Position Brazil at **technological frontier** with computational infrastructure for cutting-edge research
- Develop **LLMs in Portuguese**, based on national data
- Strengthen global leadership with **sovereignty and international sharing**

**Investment:** **BRL 23.03 billion (~USD 4 billion)** over 4 years (Budget: **R$23B** [3](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/artificial-intelligence-2025/brazil/trends-and-developments), **R$22.5B structural** [5](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S2077-72132024000200007)) [4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp).

**5 Strategic Axes:** [2](https://www.essex.ac.uk/research-projects/ai-policy-observatory-for-the-world-of-work/national-and-regional-cases/brazil)[4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp)
1. AI Infrastructure and Development
2. Diffusion, Training, and Capacity Building
3. Improvement of Public Services
4. Business Innovation
5. Support for Regulatory and Governance Process

**Funding Breakdown:**
- **~R$14B business projects:** value chain, national data centres, startups, MSMEs, industry upgrading [4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp)
- **>R$5B infrastructure & dev:** sustainable energy for data centres, high-performance computing, supercomputer **Santos Dumont upgrade**, LLM for Portuguese, national network of AI centres [4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp)[5](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S2077-72132024000200007)
- **Remaining:** training, public service, regulation — incl. **National Center for Algorithmic Transparency and Trustworthy AI** [4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp)
- **54 structural actions** (much larger budget than 31 immediate-impact actions) [5](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S2077-72132024000200007) — **42 projects** for sustainable energy (R$500M) [4](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestmentpolicy.unctad.org%2Finvestment-policy-monitor%2Fmeasures%2F4930%2Flaunches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028&hl=pt&sl=en&tl=pt&client=srp)
- **Priority enablers (KASE framework):** algorithms (50% of structural investment), data, computing capacity, education, electricity, cybersecurity — in that order [5](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S2077-72132024000200007).

**State-level:** Piauí first Secretariat of AI & Digital Economy, São Paulo pilots AI for education, Minas Gerais "Automatiza.MG" for public admin [3](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/artificial-intelligence-2025/brazil/trends-and-developments).

## 3. REGULATION — Risk-Based, EU-Style

**Current status (Sept 2024):** No comprehensive national AI regulation enacted; only subnational (Ceará). **15 bills in Senate, 9 in Chamber** [2](https://www.essex.ac.uk/research-projects/ai-policy-observatory-for-the-world-of-work/national-and-regional-cases/brazil).

**Bill No.2,338/2023 (now 2338):** National framework — principles, rights, obligations for ethical/safe/transparent AI. Includes [3](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/artificial-intelligence-2025/brazil/trends-and-developments)[1](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/brazilian-ai-strategy-2027):
- Risk classification (excessive-risk prohibited, high-risk regulated)
- Governance, accountability, human oversight
- **High-risk includes:** security devices in critical infrastructure, credit assessments, certain autonomous vehicles, healthcare, biometrics, criminal investigations/public security [3](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/artificial-intelligence-2025/brazil/trends-and-developments)
- **Excessive-risk prohibited** — aligns with call to prohibit non-MHC AWS.
- Criticism: TDM exemption for research/public interest may hinder private training [3](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/artificial-intelligence-2025/brazil/trends-and-developments).

**Other bills:** Bill 2807/2024, Bill 5005/2025, **Regulatory Sandbox** 2023 & 2025 (controlled environment to test AI for data protection compliance) [1](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/brazilian-ai-strategy-2027).

## 4. GLOBAL AI GOVERNANCE — Brazil's Hedging

**Not endorsing US military-AI club:** By 27 Nov 2024, **58 countries endorsed US Political Declaration on Responsible Military Use of AI & Autonomy** (2023 Hague REAIM Summit), mostly rich West — **Brazil, like China, did NOT appear nor support REAIM Blueprint for Action** [6](https://vanguardthinktank.org/multilateralism-no-more-the-impact-of-the-us-election-on-the-un-1). Due to high sensitivity, Brazil wants autonomous framework for Global South [6](https://vanguardthinktank.org/multilateralism-no-more-the-impact-of-the-us-election-on-the-un-1).

**Prefers:** OECD/GPAI (democratic values, human rights, accountability, transparency, diversity, inclusiveness, privacy) [6](https://vanguardthinktank.org/multilateralism-no-more-the-impact-of-the-us-election-on-the-un-1).

**Foreign policy link:** Lula plan explicitly says Brazil will play more active role in AI/digital/data norms — "digital sovereignty" with sovereign cloud [6](https://vanguardthinktank.org/multilateralism-no-more-the-impact-of-the-us-election-on-the-un-1)[ANIs]. This is *why* Brazil tables IEEE 7007 at CCW — it is extending domestic sovereignty logic to international.

**DISEC line:** "Brazil invests $4B in AI for good — our PBIA is about infrastructure, Portuguese LLM, trustworthy AI centre — we are pro-AI, pro-sovereignty, pro-human control. Regulation enables innovation, not kills it."
