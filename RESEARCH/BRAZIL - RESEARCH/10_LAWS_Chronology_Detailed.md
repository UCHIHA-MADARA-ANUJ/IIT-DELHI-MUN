## 2. KEY DOCUMENTS TO CITE (Paragraph-accurate)

### A. May 2024 SG Submission (78-241-Brazil-EN.pdf) — 10 pages
- **Humanitarian/Legal:** IHL applies to future weapons (ICJ), but AWS poses novel fundamental challenges re MHC/accountability/human dignity/distinction/proportionality/precaution.
- **MHC:** Implicit requirement in IHL (distinction etc) + IHRL. Two stems: (1) cannot program jus in bello, fails in uncertainty → without MHC violates Martens clause; (2) must ensure responsibility.
- **ICRC def:** Adopted.
- **Prohibitions:** Incompatible if cannot be understood/predicted/explained; cannot be directed at specific military objective; may cause disproportionate/superfluous injury.
- **Regulations:** For compatible AWS, operator must: impose space/time limits, approve force via situational awareness, informed reliability/predictability, take precautions not change parameters without validation, supervise + intervene via deactivation/self-destruct.
- **Arms control:** Lowers threshold, escalation, arms race, diversion to NSAs — but should not restrict transfer unduly.
- **Legal review:** Art.36 obligation to review new weapons.
- **Technical:** IEEE 7007-2021 — common platform for control mechanisms, target limits, duration, geography — Brazil WP CCW/GGE.1/2024/WP.1.
- **Conclusion:** Need normative framework — new **CCW Protocol**, but all avenues explored for Pareto-optimal earliest entry.

**Quote to memorize:** "Meaningful human control is fundamental to ensure accountability, compliance with IHL, and protection of human dignity."

### B. June 2021 Brazil-Chile-Mexico Outline (9 pages)
- IHL core obligations 5.1-5.2: distinction, incidental civilian casualties excessive vs concrete direct military advantage.
- **Prohibitions 1.1-1.3:** cannot be controlled; removes human control over critical functions; cannot comply with IHL (distinction etc).
- **Regulations 4.4-5:** limits on tasks/targets, no bias, precautionary legal reviews denying authorization if less than full certainty, verification mechanism.
- **Procedural:** Recommend GGE recommend to Sixth Review Conference start negotiations to legally binding instrument per Decision 1 of Fifth Review Conference (CCW/CONF.V/10), scope/format per Art.I as amended Second Review.
