## 1. PUBLIC OPINION — Killer Robots Poll

**Campaign to Stop Killer Robots / IPSOS poll Dec 2020:**
- **62% of Brazilians oppose lethal autonomous weapons systems** — consistent with support for legally binding instrument [1](https://plataformacipo.org/en/governance-cooperation/report-the-perception-of-the-brazilian-youth-on-lethal-autonomous-weapons-systems-killer-robots/).
- Analysis: Brazil is signatory of CCAC (CCW) and advocates legally binding instrument — position consistent with public opinion [1](https://plataformacipo.org/en/governance-cooperation/report-the-perception-of-the-brazilian-youth-on-lethal-autonomous-weapons-systems-killer-robots/).

**CIPÓ Platform Youth Report (Maiara Folly & Arthur Vieira, 2023):** [1](https://plataformacipo.org/en/governance-cooperation/report-the-perception-of-the-brazilian-youth-on-lethal-autonomous-weapons-systems-killer-robots/)
- **Report title:** "The perception of Brazilian youth on lethal autonomous weapons systems ('Killer Robots')"
- **Commissioned by:** Campaign to Stop Killer Robots (CIPÓ member)
- **Content:** Compiles/analyzes perceptions of young Brazilian leaders on risks of killer robots + role Brazilian gov should play in negotiation of international agreement restricting/regulating/prohibiting development/use.
- **Produced with support of Campaign to Stop Killer Robots.**

**Use:** "62% oppose — this is not elite Geneva, it's Brazilian youth we surveyed — democratic legitimacy for regulation."

## 2. POLARIZATION — Why Foreign Policy Matters Domestically

**ECFR 2025:** Brazil deeply polarized politically, foreign policy increasingly drawn into divide — Lula vs Bolsonaro radically different on Gaza, Bolsonaro steered away from BRICS toward Trump, climate/trade/China relations risk polarization [2](https://ecfr.eu/publication/brazil-europes-bridge-to-the-global-south/).

**Implication:** Lula uses foreign policy (G20, BRICS, COP30) to build legacy and consolidate base — needs visible Global South win (like LAWS treaty leadership).

## 3. CONSTITUTIONAL SOVEREIGNTY POLITICS

**BRICS Brasil portal 7 Aug 2025 (government-aligned):** [3](https://bricsbrasil.com.br/en/sovereignty/)
- Emphasizes constitution guarantees independence, sovereignty non-negotiable, defending sovereignty is duty of all Brazilians — voting thoughtfully, combating falsehoods, supporting national industry, defending constitution.
- **Interpretation:** National industry (Embraer/Avibras) linked to sovereignty — helps justify Harpia as sovereignty, not proliferation.

## 4. CIVIL SOCIETY — CIPÓ Platform

**CIPÓ (Platform for International Policy):** Member of Campaign to Stop Killer Robots, authored youth report. Shows civil society push for ban — government position aligns with domestic advocacy, not against.

**International activism:** Stop Killer Robots coalition (270+ CSOs, 70+ countries) shapes debate — Brazil's hosting of 2020 Rio symposium shows early gov engagement with civil society outside UN.

## 5. ELECTORAL CYCLE

- **2025:** Last year for Lula to push major policy changes before elections (Covington 2024 per Vanguard) [4](https://vanguardthinktank.org/multilateralism-no-more-the-impact-of-the-us-election-on-the-un-1). Makes 2025-2026 window for LAWS push — Lula wants deliverable.
- **2026:** PBIA investments continue, Review Conference mandate aligns with electoral cycle — Brazil wants to show tech sovereignty + humanitarian leadership.
