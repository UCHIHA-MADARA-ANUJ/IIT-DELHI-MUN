### 4.1 Indigenous Rights — Marco Temporal — 🔴 HIGH (NEW, potent)
**Fact:**
- **IACHR + UN Human Rights 17 Oct 2024:** Deep concern over surge of violence against Indigenous Peoples in Bahia, Paraná, Mato Grosso do Sul — assaults by private actors/police, forced displacement, deaths of leaders Lucas Santos de Oliveira (Dec 2023) and Nega Pataxó (Jan 2024), urged Brazil to protect territorial rights, demarcation without Marco Temporal thesis [IACHR 17 Oct 2024].
- **Federal Law 14.701/2023:** Congress passed Marco Temporal — enshrines thesis that Indigenous only have right to lands occupied at Constitution 1988, weakens rights, allows military bases/infrastructure without Free Prior Informed Consent, obstacles to recognition [Amazon Watch 19 Sept 2024].
- **Supreme Court Justice Gilmar Mendes Special Commission Aug 2024:** To define limits of Indigenous rights — APIB withdrew 25 Aug 2024 saying forum pressuring to compromise under agribusiness, Mendes allowed Marco Temporal enforced while deliberating, freezing demarcations, violence increased — especially Guarani-Kaiowá attacked by militias [Amazon Watch 19 Sept 2024].
- **HRW/Amnesty:** 652 land conflicts under review mid-2024, Guarani Kaiowá attacks Aug 2024, killing Neri Guarani Kaiowá 23yo by police Sep 2024 in Nhanderu Marangatu [Amnesty 2024].
**Attack:** "Brazil lectures on human dignity for LAWS while its Congress legalizes Marco Temporal and police kill Indigenous leaders — 652 conflicts, militias attack Guarani-Kaiowá — who are you to speak on Martens Clause?"
**Defense:** "Brazil's Constitution Art.4 prevalence of human rights is *why* Supreme Court **upheld Indigenous rights** in landmark decision — Congress reaction is *contested*, not settled law, and IACHR/UN have been **invited** — Brazil engages. For LAWS, our call for human dignity (2021 GGE) is consistent: we demand international law protect dignity *everywhere*, including at home — our youth report shows 62% demand it. We don't claim perfection, we claim commitment to law."
**One-liner:** "Rights are contested in every capital — our Court defended them, our youth demand them — that's why we want law for LAWS too."
