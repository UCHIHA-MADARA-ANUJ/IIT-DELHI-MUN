# ALGERIA — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT

## Current evidence synthesis
Algeria's existing brief says it strongly supports a legally binding instrument. The current monitored 2026 position also describes Algeria as supporting the rolling text as a solid basis for future negotiations. The main unresolved research question is how Algeria translates that legal-form support into specific prohibitions, human-control language, Article 36/legal-review requirements and verification.

## Evidence discipline
This audit separates:
- official national/UN material;
- voting history;
- CCW process participation;
- secondary monitoring.

A vote is not a complete policy platform, and historical advocacy is not automatically a current 2026 position.
