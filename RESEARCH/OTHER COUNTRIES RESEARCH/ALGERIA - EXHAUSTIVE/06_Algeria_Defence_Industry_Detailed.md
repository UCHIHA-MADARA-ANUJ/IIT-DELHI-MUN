# ALGERIA — DEFENCE INDUSTRY | Detailed (06)

**Forces:** ~130,000 active, large Russian-equipped (Su-30MKA, T-90, S-300/400, Iskander), defence budget ~$18B (highest in Africa per SIPRI 2023-24).

**LAWS program:** **No announced autonomous weapons program**, but operates modern Russian systems with increasing automation (e.g., air defence). No domestic AI weapons industry.

**Relevance:** Major importer — proliferation risk if LAWS transferred without controls. Algeria wants **transfer controls in Tier 2** and **assistance for Global South** — Brazil's OP5 transfer clause directly serves Algeria.

*Sources: SIPRI Military Expenditure, IISS, FutureOfLife (no program).*
