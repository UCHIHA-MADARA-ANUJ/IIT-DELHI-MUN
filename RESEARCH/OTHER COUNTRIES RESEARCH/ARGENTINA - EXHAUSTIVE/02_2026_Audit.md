# ARGENTINA — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT

## Current evidence synthesis
Argentina is especially important because its 2024 Secretary-General submission contains detailed substance: meaningful human control over critical functions, sufficient information to understand and assess system operation, prohibition/regulation distinction, and stronger controls on transfer/proliferation. However, the exact 2025 UNGA record in our matrix must be treated carefully because Argentina abstained on A/RES/80/57. That creates an important policy-evolution question rather than proof that Argentina abandoned earlier treaty advocacy.

## Evidence discipline
This audit separates:
- official national/UN material;
- voting history;
- CCW process participation;
- secondary monitoring.

A vote is not a complete policy platform, and historical advocacy is not automatically a current 2026 position.
