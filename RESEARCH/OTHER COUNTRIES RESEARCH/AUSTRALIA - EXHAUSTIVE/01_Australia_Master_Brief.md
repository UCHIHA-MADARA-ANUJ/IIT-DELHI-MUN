# AUSTRALIA — MASTER BRIEF | DISEC LAWS

**One-Line Stance:** **Does NOT support negotiation of legally binding instrument — "time is not yet ripe", premature, opposes parallel process** [automatedresearch Overview Aug 2024][HRW 4 Jun 2024][SafeGround Apr 2025]. **Co-author Draft Articles with Canada/Japan/Korea/UK/USA** prohibitions + regulations on basis of IHL only. **Voted FOR UNGA resolutions** Dec 2023 78/241, Dec 2024 79/62 (161-3-13 First Committee → 166-3-15 GA) [HRW Dec 2024] but **curtailed ambition of 2024 resolution** seeking to reduce scope [SafeGround Apr 2025].

**Detailed:**
- **SG submission 24 May 2024 (78-241-Australia-EN, A/79/88):** Advocates continued discussion within CCW GGE to **build shared understanding of how existing law applies before pursuing any new legal instrument** [Overview]. **Does not support standard of human control** — argues **"no express IHL requirement for weapon to be subject to human control"** [Overview][CJIL]. Prefers **AWS not LAWS** (since some effects non-lethal) [Overview]. Says control is **means to comply with IHL, not separate requirement** [ReachingCriticalWill Vol 13 No3 Jun 2025]. Distinguishes **control vs judgment**: control = applied in design/development/testing/setting parameters to determine effects; judgment = decision-making of commanders [ReachingCriticalWill]. Says contemporaneous human control **not necessarily required during selection/engagement** to comply with IHL — depends on context [ReachingCriticalWill].
- **GGE 2020 commentary:** Lawful? Compliance via Article 36 reviews, IHL fully applies, human responsibility retained, autonomy may help enhance compliance/precision [Australia 20 Aug 2020].
- **Civil society pushback:** 200 Australian AI experts letter 2017, Human Rights Commissioner, SafeGround primer says majority of states 129 support binding instrument while Australia insists not, contrasts with landmines/clusters/biological leadership [HRW 4 Jun 2024][SafeGround].

**Defence AI:** Investing in autonomy-R&D across Defence, ADF, universities [SafeGround].

**Bloc:** HARD SOFT-HEDGE with USA/UK/Canada/Japan/Korea — **most doctrinally opposed to human-control-as-law**.
