## Current 2026 assessment
Australia voted YES on A/RES/80/57 and remains active in CCW working groups. Its historical statements have been cautious about prematurely regulating autonomy and have emphasized IHL and the existing legal framework. Current 2026 research should focus on whether its position has shifted after the 2026 GGE elements and whether it accepts a binding instrument.
