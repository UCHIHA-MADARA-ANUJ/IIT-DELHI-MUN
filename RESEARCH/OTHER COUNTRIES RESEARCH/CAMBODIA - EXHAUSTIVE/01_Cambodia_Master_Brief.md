# CAMBODIA — LAWS COUNTRY DOSSIER
> Exact committee matrix | Brazil DISEC research | September 2026

## 1. Current position
Supports negotiation of a legally binding instrument. Cambodia is a CCW State Party and member of ASEAN and NAM. In 2025, ASEAN statement language delivered by Cambodia called for a robust, future-proof, legally binding instrument and highlighted arms-race, escalation and non-state proliferation risks.

## 2. Evidence status
Current monitored position: binding; strong ASEAN context.

## 3. Negotiating strengths
ASEAN platform; direct participation in CCW; clear regional statement supporting a legal instrument.

## 4. Constraints / research vulnerabilities

## 5. Brazil-specific research questions
Explore ASEAN priorities: proliferation, lowering conflict thresholds, inclusive dialogue, and what “future-proof” regulation should mean in practice.

## 6. 2025 UNGA record
