# CHILE — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT

## Current evidence synthesis
Chile is a long-standing pro-binding state and Brazil's historic partner. Its 2024 submission emphasizes unpredictability, explainability, restrictions in populated areas and a legally binding additional protocol. Chile also participated in the Brazil-led 2025 rolling-text group. The key audit is to compare Chile's exact prohibited-system test with Brazil's, especially on human targeting, unpredictability and lifecycle controls.

## Evidence discipline
Separate official national/UN positions, voting history, CCW process participation and secondary monitoring. A vote is not a complete policy platform, and historical advocacy is not automatically a current 2026 position.
