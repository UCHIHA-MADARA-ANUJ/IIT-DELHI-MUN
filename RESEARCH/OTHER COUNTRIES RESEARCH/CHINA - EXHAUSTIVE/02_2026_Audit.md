> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
China abstained on all three recent UNGA LAWS resolutions, including A/RES/80/57. Its 2024 SG submission supports continued negotiation in the CCW and a categorized approach, but argues that only systems meeting a cumulative set of particularly severe characteristics should be prohibited. China also prefers ‘appropriate human involvement’ language and insists conditions for a binding outcome must mature.
