# COLOMBIA — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT

## Current evidence synthesis
Colombia is a strong treaty-supporting state and one of the authors of the Draft Protocol VI architecture. Its 2023 statement called for a binding instrument prohibiting fully autonomous systems and regulating others with significant human control. Colombia also joined the 42-state rolling-text paper. The next research layer is exact clause language and the degree of overlap with Brazil's definition and enforcement proposals.

## Evidence discipline
Separate official national/UN positions, voting history, CCW process participation and secondary monitoring. A vote is not a complete policy platform, and historical advocacy is not automatically a current 2026 position.
