# CUBA — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT

## Current evidence synthesis
Cuba has a long-running pro-binding record, including early calls for a ban. The existing repository evidence is partly based on secondary/advocacy sources, so the current research standard requires replacing those where possible with Cuban UN/CCW statements and current 2026 material. Do not use the older 'ban-call states' label as if it were an official Cuban taxonomy.

## Evidence discipline
Separate official national/UN positions, voting history, CCW process participation and secondary monitoring. A vote is not a complete policy platform, and historical advocacy is not automatically a current 2026 position.
