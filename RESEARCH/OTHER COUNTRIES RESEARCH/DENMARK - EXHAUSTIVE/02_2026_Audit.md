# DENMARK — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT

## Current evidence synthesis
Denmark's existing brief records consistent support for a binding instrument. Its more useful research identity is as an EU/European state that joined the 42-HCP rolling-text paper and therefore supports moving the GGE text forward. The next audit should identify Denmark's preferred prohibition criteria, human-control language and relationship between EU and national positions.

## Evidence discipline
Separate official national/UN positions, voting history, CCW process participation and secondary monitoring. A vote is not a complete policy platform, and historical advocacy is not automatically a current 2026 position.
