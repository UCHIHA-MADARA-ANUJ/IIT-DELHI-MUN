# ECUADOR — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT

## Current evidence synthesis
Ecuador has unusually strong historical credentials: it was the second state identified by HRW as calling for a ban in 2014 and became a Draft Protocol VI co-author. Its position combines IHL, human dignity/right-to-life and concern that life-and-death decisions should not be delegated to autonomous systems. The key audit is to update this historical position with current 2025/2026 statements and compare its Draft Protocol VI language with Brazil's.

## Evidence discipline
Separate official national/UN positions, voting history, CCW process participation and secondary monitoring. A vote is not a complete policy platform, and historical advocacy is not automatically a current 2026 position.
