# EGYPT — MASTER BRIEF | DISEC LAWS

**One-Line Stance:** **STRONGLY SUPPORTS legally binding instrument — third country globally to call for ban** [FutureOfLife Guide 17 Sep 2024][autonomousweapons.org]. Two-tier approach: prohibition of fully autonomous weapons while regulating other military AI applications most realistic/effective [Egypt SG submission May 2024][Overview Aug 2024].

**Detailed:**
- **SG submission May 2024 (78-241-Egypt-EN, A/79/88 42-44):** Rapid development alarming — requires urgent action including legally binding prohibition of fully autonomous as suggested by SG New Agenda for Peace [Egypt SG]. Says systems whose behavior cannot be reasonably predicted/understood/explained should also fall within prohibition — includes lack explainability/traceability, operational effects cannot be foreseen, post-deployment self-learning/adaptive capabilities beyond human oversight [Egypt GGE 5 Mar 2026]. Underscores **meaningful human control**, all military AI must remain fully compliant with international law, preserve accountability/legal responsibility [Egypt SG]. Term **fully autonomous weapons** should exclusively mean systems without human intervention plus effective verification [Overview]. Warns against arbitrary oversight/discriminatory controls, streamline international AI military guidelines under UN [Overview].
- **GGE 9 Apr 2018:** View legally binding instrument against development/manufacture urgent, plus regulation of existing systems [Egypt 9 Apr 2018].
