# FRANCE — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT
> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
France joined the 42-state process and supports a two-tier architecture: prohibit systems that cannot comply with IHL; regulate other systems through lifecycle measures. France's 2024 submission supports an additional CCW protocol and a technical-expert mechanism. It is therefore important to distinguish France's strong support for substantive rules from the exact timetable/legal text it will accept.
