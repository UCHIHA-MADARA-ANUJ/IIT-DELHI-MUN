# GERMANY — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT
> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
Germany supports a legally binding framework, has participated in the 42-state process, and supports a two-tier approach with human control and lifecycle regulation. Its 2024 SG submission distinguishes autonomous systems outside human control from automatic defensive systems and emphasizes human responsibility. The exact scope of its preferred prohibitions remains important to compare with Brazil.
