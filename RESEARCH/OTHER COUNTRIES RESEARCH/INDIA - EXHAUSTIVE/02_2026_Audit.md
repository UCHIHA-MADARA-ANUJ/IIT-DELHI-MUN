# INDIA — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT
> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
India voted YES on A/RES/80/57 after abstaining in 2024. Its 2024 explanation emphasized CCW centrality, definitions/characterization and avoiding duplicate processes. India's more recent working-level position emphasizes technology neutrality, IHL compliance, human judgment/oversight, development access and a gradual approach; it has distinguished fully autonomous systems that cannot comply with IHL from systems retained under human involvement.
