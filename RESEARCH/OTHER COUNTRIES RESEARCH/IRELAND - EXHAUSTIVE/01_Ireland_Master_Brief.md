# IRELAND — MASTER BRIEF | DISEC LAWS

**One-Line Stance:** **STRONGLY SUPPORTS legally binding instrument — urgency, bias/IHRL/IHL/IHL criminal law** [Ireland SG submission + automatedresearch Ireland 12 Nov 2024][Overview Aug 2024]. Co-sponsor L.56 2023, L.77 2024, 80/57 2025, 42-state Sept 2025, Vienna 2024. Warned 10 Oct 2022: failure to make progress at CCW GGE cannot continue indefinitely [Ireland 10 Oct 2022].

**Detailed:**
- **SG submission 2024:** Supports legally binding solution, underlines urgency, underscores legal/ethical/bias risks, calls for comprehensive framework [Overview]. Warns **consequences of bias in machine learning amplified in military context — women of colour may be misrecognized at higher rate leaving differential risks, system may miscategorise civilian men as combatants due to traditional roles** [CJIL citing Ireland 146]. Insists autonomous weapons must comply fully with IHL/IHRL/criminal law, necessity for human judgement due to cognitive limitations/algorithmic biases [Overview]. Argues **functional characterisation**: autonomy is feature of functionality rather than weapon type — governance should focus on prohibition/regulation of integration of autonomy, not fixed definition based on technical characteristics [Overview].
- **GGE 2023:** "It is clear certain systems require regulation with clear rules and others should be prohibited. Autonomous weapons that cannot be used in accordance with IHL must not be developed. Ceding human control/agency/responsibility to algorithms should not be acceptable" [automatedresearch citing 2023].
- **Dáil written answer 9 Apr 2025:** Favours **two-tier approach** — prohibit development/use of systems which could not be used in full compliance with IHL/IHRL, regulate others by ensuring meaningful human control/accountability [Oireachtas 9 Apr 2025]. Primary forum CCW GGE but notes tech developments risk outpacing consensus; supports US-led Political Declaration on Responsible Military Use of AI, participated at REAIM The Hague 2023 + Seoul 2024 [Oireachtas].

**Votes:** Co-sponsored + FOR all.

**Bloc:** CORE SUPPORTER — European moral authority with Austria.
