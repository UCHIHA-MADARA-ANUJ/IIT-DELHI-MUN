> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
Israel voted NO on A/RES/80/57. Its current LAWS position should be researched from its own statements rather than from descriptions of its military operations. The main policy questions are whether it considers existing IHL and national reviews sufficient, how it defines human control, and whether it accepts specific prohibitions or only regulation.
