Described by former intel officer as "mass assassination factory" where emphasis on quantity not quality. Bombs judged by number generated. Infrastructure bombing to create shock, pressure on Hamas [Palestine submission 24 May 2024, +972 Investigations, CIVICUS Dec 2024].
- **Lavender:** AI program to generate **human** kill lists — marked **37,000 Palestinians** as "suspected militants." Treat AI recommendation "as if it were human decision," **no requirement to thoroughly check why machine chose.** Only mandatory check: ensure target male — takes **20 seconds**.
