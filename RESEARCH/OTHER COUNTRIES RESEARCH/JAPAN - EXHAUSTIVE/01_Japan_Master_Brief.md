# JAPAN — MASTER BRIEF | DISEC LAWS

**One-Line Stance:** **Does NOT support negotiation of legally binding instrument — says premature** [automatedresearch Japan 12 Nov 2024][Overview Aug 2024]. But **voted FOR all UNGA resolutions** Dec 2023 78/241, Dec 2024 79/L.77, Dec 2025 80/57 [automatedresearch]. **Party to CCW**.

**Position Detailed — Human-centric but not binding:**
- **May 2024 submission to UNSG (78-241-Japan-rev-EN):** Defines LAWS as "once activated, can identify, select, engage targets with lethal force without further intervention by operator" [AA 15 Jul 2024][Japan submission]. Says **"does not intend to develop autonomous weapons systems with lethal force that operate completely without human involvement"** at least at present moment [Japan submission 8pp]. Insists **human involvement required — humans not machines accountable under IHL**, traceability of responsibility [Japan submission]. But argues **universality requires stakeholder states with high tech to participate, supports CCW continuation** [Japan submission]. Distinguishes 2 categories: (a) weapons inherently indiscriminate/incapable of IHL compliance must not be used (b) other systems should be restricted via controlling types of targets, duration, geographical scope, scale, self-destruct/deactivation [Japan submission]. Advocates **human-centric principle, reliability/predictability, not controlling humans** [Japan submission].
- **AA 15 Jul 2024:** Submitted policy to UN May 2024, senior ministry official says "currently no assurances LAWS will comply with IHL, does not intend to develop" [AA].
- **Draft Articles co-author:** Australia, Canada, Japan, Poland, Republic of Korea, UK, USA submitted **Draft articles on autonomous weapon systems – prohibitions and other regulatory measures on basis of IHL** (CCW/GGE.1/2023/WP.4/Rev.1) — **prohibitions + regulations within IHL**, not new legally binding ban [Japan explanation 1 Nov 2023].
- **Premature claim:** At CCW GGE 21 Sep 2020 said negotiation of legally binding instrument would be "premature" [automatedresearch citing]. Accepts that existing IHL obligations apply, weapon systems must not be used if incapable of IHL compliance (2023 GGE report) [Japan GGE 2024 CRP1].

**Votes:** **FOR all** — so joins consensus even while saying premature. Explains vote 1 Nov 2023 L.56: welcomes resolution under Austria, CCW is most appropriate forum, GGE remains best [estatements Japan].

**Defence:** Self-Defense Forces facing recruitment challenges — sees benefits of autonomy for reducing human error, manpower [Tribune 15 Jul 2024].

**Bloc:** SOFT P5-ALIGNED HEDGE (Group C) — with South Korea, Canada, UK, USA, Australia — **co-sponsors Draft Articles**, wants CCW only, no new treaty.
