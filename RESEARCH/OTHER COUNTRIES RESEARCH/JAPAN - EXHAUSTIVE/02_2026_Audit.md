# JAPAN — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT
> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
Japan voted YES on A/RES/80/57 and has an official working definition focused on systems that, once activated, can identify, select and engage targets without additional operator intervention. Japan favors consensus-based work through the CCW, but its public posture does not amount to unconditional support for Brazil's preferred treaty architecture. Definitions and automatic defensive systems are key technical points.
