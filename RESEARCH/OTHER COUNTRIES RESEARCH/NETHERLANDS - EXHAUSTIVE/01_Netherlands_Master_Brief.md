# NETHERLANDS — MASTER BRIEF | DISEC LAWS

**One-Line Stance:** **STRONGLY SUPPORTS negotiation of legally binding instrument** — first indicated June 2022 letter to parliament, Cabinet response to AIV/CAVV advice [automatedresearch Netherlands 14 Nov 2024]. **Committed to two-tier approach**: prohibit AWS that cannot be used in accordance with IHL must be explicitly prohibited, while others regulated — objective ensure sufficient human judgment/control [Netherlands GGE 6 Mar 2023][Netherlands SG submission 78-241-Netherlands-EN].

**Detailed:**
- **17 Jun 2022 Cabinet response:** Pursue international effort to ban autonomous weapons via CCW protocol [FutureOfLife 17 Sep 2024].
- **SG submission May 2024 (78-241-Netherlands-EN):** Starting point AWS must be designed/developed/used in accordance with international law, certain level of human judgment/control retained throughout lifecycle. AWS that cannot be designed/developed/used in accordance with IHL must be explicitly prohibited through legally binding instrument, preferably adding **Protocol VI to CCW** [Netherlands SG]. General characterization: "weapons system able to, after [activation]..." inherently unpredictable outside responsible chain of command subject to prohibition [Netherlands SG]. Existing IHL applies but specifying norms helps states comply; categories for prohibition: designed to apply force against civilians/civilian objects etc [Netherlands SG].
- **REAIM:** Initiated from Dutch parliamentary resolution, 60 countries 2023 call to action (non-binding) [FutureOfLife].

**Votes:** FOR all UNGA resolutions Dec 2023, Dec 2024, Dec 2025.

**Bloc:** CORE SUPPORTER — natural ally of Brazil.

*Sources: automatedresearch Netherlands 14 Nov 2024, Netherlands SG submission 78-241-Netherlands-EN, FutureOfLife 17 Sep 2024, OpinioJuris 4 Mar 2024.*
