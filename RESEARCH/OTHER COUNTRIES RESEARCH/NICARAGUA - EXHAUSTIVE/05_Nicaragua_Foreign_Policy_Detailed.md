# NICARAGUA — FOREIGN POLICY DNA | Detailed (05)

**Identity:** Small Central American state, ALBA member (with Cuba/Venezuela/Bolivia), historically anti-US intervention stance, close to Russia/China for economic/military support but votes with Global South majority on disarmament.

**Why LAWS matters:** Nicaragua has no LAWS program; its security concern is **proliferation and use by powerful states against weaker states**, and defense of IHL/IHRL principles (consistent with its ICJ activism — Nicaragua v. USA 1986, recent ICJ cases on Gaza).

**LAWS Diplomacy:**
- Listed among **30 states calling for ban on autonomous weapons since 2013-2015** (FutureOfLife pledge list includes Nicaragua). This is long-standing ban-call position.
- Yet **abstained on UNGA 79/62 (L.77) Dec 2024 — among 15 abstain** (HRW Dec 2024: China, Estonia, Fiji, India, Iran, Israel, Latvia, Lithuania, **Nicaragua**, Poland, Romania, Saudi, Syria, Turkey, Ukraine) — paradox.
- **Explanation for paradox:** Nicaragua's abstention in 2024 was **not rejection of binding instrument** but protest against **US/Russia dominance of text** and alignment with Russia/China bloc hedging? Actually Nicaragua typically votes with Russia on disarmament when US is opponent — but here Russia voted AGAINST, Nicaragua abstained. Need to distinguish: Nicaragua supports ban in principle but abstained on L.77 because of concerns about scope/definition? For committee, treat as **STRONGLY SUPPORTS binding instrument** (30-list) with tactical abstention — you can cite both: "Nicaragua called for ban since 2015, voted FOR 78/241 (2023), but abstained 166-3-15 (2024) — why the shift?"

**Brazil angle:** Nicaragua is natural ally for Brazil's 42-state statement if you can pull it back from abstention to FOR in committee. Offer language on non-intervention and Global South capacity-building (Nicaragua cares about sovereignty).
