# NICARAGUA — DEFENCE INDUSTRY & FORCES | Detailed (06)

**Forces:** Small army (~12,000 active), obsolete equipment, defence budget ~$90M (0.6% GDP). No domestic defence industry.

**LAWS relevance:** **Zero LAWS program.** Imports Russian equipment (T-72 tanks, patrol boats). Relies on Russian/Chinese support.

**Why this matters for DISEC:**
- Nicaragua is **pure Global South victim-case**: has no means to develop LAWS, but would be vulnerable if LAWS proliferate to neighbors or non-state actors. Its interest is **preventive prohibition** before asymmetric disadvantage widens.
- Can be used by Brazil to argue proliferation: "Nicaragua, with no autonomous capability, still abstained in 2024 — because even states without programs fear being on receiving end of autonomous strikes without rules."

**No autonomous systems deployed.** No AI strategy.

*Sources: SIPRI, IISS Military Balance (Nicaragua forces), FutureOfLife (no program).*
