**Detailed:**
- **SG submission 24 May 2024 (78-241-Norway-EN, 4pp):** Primary aim in GGE: clarify application of IHL. **Remains agnostic on specific form but protocol under CCW desirable** [Norway SG]. **Supports two-tier**: (1) systems operating **completely outside human control and responsible chain of command** (capable of setting own objectives/modifying without human validation) — must be **regarded as already prohibited under existing IHL**, but prohibition should be made explicit; (2) systems **featuring decision-making autonomy in critical functions** (identification/classification/interception/engagement to which military command can assign computation) — may be allowable **only if** subject to **meaningful human control** that is also **relevant** (substantive input sufficient to ensure IHL compliance) [Norway SG]. Prohibited if: inherently indiscriminate/incapable of distinction, effects cannot be limited/anticipated/controlled, superfluous injury, otherwise incapable of IHL compliance [Norway SG]. For category 2, requires framework ensuring reliability/understandability/predictability, control/limit types of targets, duration/geographical scope/scale, **preserve human control at all times across entire lifecycle**, humans must have understanding, predict/explain behaviour, define/validate rules of engagement, monitor reliability, approve modification, exercise judgment on distinction/proportionality/precautions [Norway SG]. Believe targeting humans raises particular questions must be addressed [Norway SG].
