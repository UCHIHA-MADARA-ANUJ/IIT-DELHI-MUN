# PAKISTAN — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT
> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
Pakistan is in the 42-state Brazil-led process and is an unusually active GGE participant. Research should focus on how Pakistan reconciles support for negotiations with its security concerns, its preferred definition, and the balance between prohibitions, regulation, human control and national defence requirements.
