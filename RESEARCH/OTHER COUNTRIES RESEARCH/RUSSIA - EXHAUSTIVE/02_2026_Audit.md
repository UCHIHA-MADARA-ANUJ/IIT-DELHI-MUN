# RUSSIA — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT
> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
Russia voted NO on A/RES/80/57 and has repeatedly opposed movement toward a new legally binding LAWS instrument. Its 2024 SG submission rejects some human-control terminology as political and emphasizes operator responsibility, lifecycle testing, reliability, training, continuous monitoring, emergency termination and preventing transfer to non-state actors. This means Russia's position is not simply ‘no rules’; it supports regulation through existing law and lifecycle controls while resisting the treaty architecture Brazil favors.
