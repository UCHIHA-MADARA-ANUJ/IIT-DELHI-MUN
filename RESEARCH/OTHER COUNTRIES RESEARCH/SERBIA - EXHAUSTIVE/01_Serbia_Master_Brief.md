# SERBIA — LAWS COUNTRY DOSSIER
> Exact committee matrix | Brazil DISEC research | September 2026

## 1. Current position
Serbia made an unusually detailed 2024 Secretary-General submission. It said LAWS should be prohibited or their production restricted and use monitored; it highlighted collateral damage, responsibility gaps, arms races, hacking, non-state possession and swarms. It argued for strict regulation and international conventions.

## 2. Evidence status
Strong humanitarian/regulatory concern documented; exact 2026 legal-form evolution still needs checking.

## 3. Negotiating strengths
Primary national submission is unusually explicit; conventional-weapons treaty engagement; concern about command responsibility and proliferation.

## 4. Constraints / research vulnerabilities
Its statement dates from May 2024, so it must not be presented automatically as the unchanged 2026 position.

## 5. Brazil-specific research questions
Serbia's 2024 submission creates substantial factual overlap with Brazil on control, monitoring, responsibility and proliferation. The key research question is whether Serbia now supports a formally binding instrument.

## 6. 2025 UNGA record
The official vote on A/RES/80/57 was recorded on 1 December 2025. The vote and sponsor information should be read together with the country's national position; a YES vote is not itself proof of support for every possible treaty mechanism.
