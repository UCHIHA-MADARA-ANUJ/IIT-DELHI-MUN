# SRI LANKA — LAWS COUNTRY DOSSIER
> Exact committee matrix | Brazil DISEC research | September 2026

## 1. Current position
Strongly supports a legally binding instrument. In March 2026 Sri Lanka said the rolling text contained fundamental elements for future negotiations and, in September 2026, its Permanent Representative called for an explicit negotiating mandate. Sri Lanka emphasizes human judgment/control throughout the lifecycle and strict prohibitions on systems outside human control or targeting humans directly.

## 2. Evidence status
Current official position: strong support for a binding negotiating mandate.

## 3. Negotiating strengths

## 4. Constraints / research vulnerabilities

## 5. Brazil-specific research questions
