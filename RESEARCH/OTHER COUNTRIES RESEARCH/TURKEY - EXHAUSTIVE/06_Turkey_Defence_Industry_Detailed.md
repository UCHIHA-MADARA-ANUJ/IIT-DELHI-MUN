# TURKEY — DEFENCE INDUSTRY | Detailed
> File 06

**Company: STM (Savunma Teknolojileri Mühendisliği) — state-owned defense co., maker of Kargu-2.**
- **Kargu-2:** Rotary-wing kamikaze/loitering munition, designed for asymmetric/anti-terror operations [Ploughshares]. Capabilities per STM: "fire, forget and find" [EurAsian Times], ML-based object classification to select/engage targets [Bulletin 20 May 2021], swarm capabilities 20 drones together in development [Bulletin], "anti-personnel" in STM video diving onto manikins [Bulletin].
- **Operation:** Autonomous navigation + target differentiation (human/animal/vehicle per STM CEO) [Ploughshares]. STM CEO Ozgur Guleryuz denies fully autonomous attack — says autonomy mostly navigation + differentiation; operator must manually verify and press button, which can be cancelled any time [EurAsian Times][Ploughshares]. Turkish delegate at CCW informal June 28-July 2 2021 reiterated UN Panel wrong, munition is remotely controlled [Ploughshares].
- **UN Panel Finding:** 8 Mar 2021 report: KARGU-2 "hunted down and remotely engaged" soldiers of Haftar Affiliated Forces — programmed to attack without data connectivity between operator and munition: true "fire, forget and find" [EurAsian Times][Ploughshares][Bulletin].
- **Uncertainty:** Not clear if killed anyone — report implies losses but not confirmed; at least proves *capability and deployment* [Ploughshares]. If swarming, central question is how targets selected [Ploughshares].

**Bayraktar TB2:** Baykar, provides autonomous flight with AI-assisted target acquisition, widely exported [Hive Security Jun 2026].

**Industry motive:** Turkey is **top drone exporter** — wants to avoid treaty that stigmatizes loitering munitions or requires human control per engagement (would kill "fire and forget").

**Brazil POI weaponization:** Use Kargu-2 as Exhibit A for Tier 1 prohibition (unpredictable/indiscriminate). If Turkey says "our drone needs button," reply: "Then you should have no problem codifying button requirement in treaty — why abstain?"

*Sources: EurAsian Times 22 Jun 2021, Ploughshares 15 Jul 2021, Bulletin 20 May 2021, Hive Security Jun 2026, UN Panel S/2021/229.*
