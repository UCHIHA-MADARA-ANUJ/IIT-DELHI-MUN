# UNITED KINGDOM — 2026 LAWS RESEARCH AUDIT
> This file updates/qualifies older dossier material. It is not a replacement for the historical record.

## Current 2026 assessment
The UK voted YES on A/RES/80/57. Its official posture has emphasized that fully autonomous weapons are unacceptable, while accepting lawful military uses of AI and autonomy under context-appropriate human involvement and legal reviews. It is active in CCW discussions but has not adopted Brazil's full treaty-first position. The research priority is the exact boundary between unacceptable autonomy and acceptable supervised autonomy.
