## Current 2026 assessment
The United States voted NO on A/RES/80/57. Its earlier A/79/88 submission and GGE statements argue that existing IHL and national weapons-review measures can address the issue and that the time is not right for a new legally binding instrument. The U.S. has separately promoted a Political Declaration on Responsible Military Use of AI and Autonomy. The 2025/2026 record should be treated as a continuing preference for existing-law/voluntary or political mechanisms rather than a treaty-first approach.
