# VENEZUELA — FOREIGN POLICY DNA | Detailed (05)

**Identity:** Bolivarian Republic, ALBA leader, anti-US sanctions, close military/political alignment with Russia/China/Iran/Cuba, but historically active in CELAC/NAM disarmament.

**LAWS Diplomacy:**
- Among **30 states calling for ban** (FutureOfLife — Venezuela listed) since 2015. Consistent ban advocate.
- **Voted FOR all UNGA LAWS resolutions:** 78/241 (2023), 79/62 (2024) — Venezuela voted FOR 166, not abstain, despite Russian ally Russia voting NO. This shows **autonomy from Russia** on this file — important distinction from Nicaragua/Belarus.
- **CELAC/NAM statements:** Supports legally binding instrument, references IHL and human rights.

**Tension:** Sanctions and economic crisis mean Venezuela has no LAWS program but frames LAWS as **imperialist weapon** of powerful states — wants prohibition to constrain US/Israel. Good ally for Brazil's Tier 1 ban.

**Brazil angle:** Venezuela is natural co-sponsor for prohibition, but its close Russia ties make it wary of CCW vs UNGA track (Russia wants CCW consensus to block). Brazil should emphasize **42-state cross-regional statement includes Global South + Europe** — gives Venezuela cover to vote with Brazil, not just Russia.

*Sources: FutureOfLife 30 list (Venezuela), press.un.org/ga12736 (Venezuela not in NO/abstain), HRW 2024 (Venezuela not in 15 abstain — voted FOR).*
