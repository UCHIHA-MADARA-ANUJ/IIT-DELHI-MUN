# VENEZUELA — DEFENCE INDUSTRY | Detailed (06)

**Forces:** ~125,000 active, Russian-equipped (Su-30MK2, T-72, S-300VM), economic collapse degraded readiness.

**LAWS program:** **None.** No AI strategy, no autonomous systems. Some Russian drones imported but not autonomous targeting.

**Relevance:** Classic **have-not** state: vulnerable to autonomous proliferation, wants prohibition before technology widens gap. Also concerned about **autonomous border surveillance by Colombia/US**.
