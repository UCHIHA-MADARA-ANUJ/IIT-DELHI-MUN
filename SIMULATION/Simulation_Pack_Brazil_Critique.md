# SIMULATION PACK — BRAZIL DISEC LAWS | PRACTICE UNDER FIRE

> How to use: Read the committee prompt, deliver your speech/POI/answer out loud (60-90 sec), then read critique. Best practiced with a friend as chair/USA/India.

---

## ROUND 1: GSL — FIRST IMPRESSION

**Chair:** "Brazil, you have 90 seconds, opening speech."

*Deliver your opening (use GSL/GSL_Speeches.md). Then self-score:*

**Critique checklist:**
- Did you say "no algorithm should decide who lives and who dies" in first 10 sec? (If you started with "Honourable chair..." — rewrite)
- Did you cite 166 votes + 42 states + IEEE 7007 or Article 36? (0 citations = average; 2+ = Good; 3 = Best)
- Did you propose Protocol VI + two-tier + reviews? (Solution, not just problem)
- Did you end with challenge ("will this committee let one state veto 166?") not thank-you summary?

**Weak answer example to avoid:** "Brazil believes LAWS are very dangerous and we need human control and international cooperation..." (No facts, no numbers, forgettable.)

---

## ROUND 2: POI — OFFENSIVE (YOU ASK)

**You to USA:** US just said IHL is enough.

Your POI: "The US voted against 2025 LAWS resolution with Russia and Israel while 164 voted for, and its own Directive 3000.09 requires human judgment over use of force — why oppose codifying its own standard?"

- **Good follow if USA says "need flexibility":** "Flexibility for what — a system that cannot be aborted before striking? Tier 2 regulation keeps flexibility under human control."

**You to India:** India says strategic autonomy, abstained.

Your POI: "India abstained with China while 164 advanced — autonomy without rules is autonomy for NSAGs who could acquire swarms. What verifiable standard does India propose instead?"

---

## ROUND 3: POI — DEFENSIVE (THEY HIT YOU)

**India to you:** "Brazil is in BRICS with Russia and China who oppose this treaty. Is Brazil coherent? And Brazil never joined the Cluster Munitions Convention — why should we trust Brazil on CCW protocols?"

**Bad answer (defensive, rambling):** "Brazil has its own foreign policy... we support disarmament..."

**Best answer (3-part, 45 sec):**
> "On BRICS: BRICS is not a military alliance, it is a platform for development. Brazil exercises principled autonomy — we differ from partners on mines, on nuclear weapons, and today on LAWS because law requires it. That autonomy makes us a bridge, not a bloc loyalist. On cluster munitions: Brazil favored a universal CCW with teeth over a treaty outside CCW with interoperability loopholes in Article 21 that let users keep using through partners. For LAWS we propose exactly the opposite mistake avoided — a CCW Protocol VI, universal and binding, annexing IEEE standards so there is no loophole. Brazil's cost is zero LAWS program, so we have no inventory to protect — that is why we can be honest broker, while SISFRON shows we use autonomy for monitoring under human control every day."

**Critique:** Did you answer both parts? Did you turn weakness into strength (no programme = credibility; SISFRON = responsible use)? Did you stay diplomatic, not attack India back?

## ROUND 4: MOD CAUCUS — THROWN TOPIC

Deliver MOD A speech (GSL pack). Self-score: Did you define MHC operationally (understand/predict/explain/abort) + cite Martens + propose space/time limits? If you only said "human control is important" — redo with definition.

## ROUND 5: UNMOD — LOBBYING TEST

**Scenario:** You approach Germany who says: "We like regulation, not ban. Tier 1 is too broad."

Your line: "Tier 1 is only human targeting and unpredictable/indiscriminate systems. Your systems with human approval stay in Tier 2 under control. If we add 'with appropriate human judgment' to OP2, can Germany co-sponsor OP2?"

**Critique:** Did you offer Germany authorship (OP2 credit) instead of arguing? Did you narrow Tier 1 to make it acceptable? That's how you pull fence-sitters.
