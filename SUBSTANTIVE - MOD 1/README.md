## SUBSTANCE FOR TWO-TIER

**Tier 1 Prohibitions (cite Brazil-Chile-Mexico 2021 outline + Brazil 2024 submission):**
- Cannot be meaningfully controlled
- Cannot be understood/predicted/explained
- Cannot be directed at specific military objective (violates distinction)
- May cause disproportionate harm / superfluous injury
- Autonomously targets humans

**Tier 2 Regulations (positive obligations):**
- Human approval of each engagement with situational awareness
- Strict geographic + temporal limits
- Ability to supervise, intervene, deactivate/self-destruct
- No change of mission parameters without human validation
- Logging + certification

**Source lines to quote:**
- "Without meaningful human control... would undermine IHL and violate human dignity" (Brazil, GGE 2021)
- "Prohibitions and regulations while fully respecting IHL" (Brazil Explanation of Vote 2023)

**Operative language for resolution:**
> *OP1: Prohibits development, production, transfer and use of AWS that...*
> *OP2: Requires meaningful human control over critical functions, including...*
