## SUBSTANCE FOR MHC & IHL

**Why MHC is implicit in IHL (Brazil 2024 submission):**
- Distinction: requires human judgment to distinguish combatant/civilian, active/hors de combat
- Proportionality: "excessive in relation to concrete and direct military advantage" — algorithm cannot weigh context
- Precaution: must take precautions to avoid civilian harm
- Military necessity: cannot be coded into uncertain environments

**Legal anchors:**
- **ICJ:** "IHL applies to all forms of warfare and all kinds of weapons, past present future"
- **Martens Clause:** civilians/combatants remain under protection of principles of humanity + dictates of public conscience — delegating kill to code violates this
- **Article 36 AP I:** "High Contracting Parties shall determine whether employment of new weapon would be prohibited by IHL"
- **Human dignity:** "Robots should not have power of life and death" (Brazil, HRC 2013)

**Test for legality:** Can operator (a) understand, (b) predict, (c) explain, (d) limit in space/time, (e) abort? If no → prohibit.

**Example line:** "An AWS that cannot be aborted is inherently incompatible with IHL — Brazil's 2024 submission says so explicitly."
