# SUBSTANTIVE - MOD 3 — Substance for MOD 3: Technical Standards

**File:** `Substantive_MOD3.md`

## SUBSTANCE FOR VERIFIABILITY

**Brazil's unique contribution:**
- **Working Paper CCW/GGE.1/2024/WP.1** — Brazil tabled IEEE STD 7007-2021 (first global ontological standard for ethically driven robotics)
- What it does: common platform for core concepts — control mechanisms, limits on targets, duration, geographical scope

**Why it matters:**
- Answers US/UK objection "can't define AWS"
- Makes Article 36 reviews comparable (currently secret, divergent)
- Enables logging, certification, cooperation

**Article 36 text to quote:**
> "In the study, development, acquisition or adoption of a new weapon, means or method of warfare, a High Contracting Party is under an obligation to determine whether its employment would, in some or all circumstances, be prohibited by this Protocol or by any other rule of international law."

**Brazil's logic:** If Article 36 already requires review, adding IEEE annex REDUCES legal risk for developers/militaries — not increases burden.

**Proposed resolution language:**
> *Requires states to certify AWS via IEEE 7007-based technical annex, log engagement decisions, ensure deactivation capability, and cooperate on verification; establishes assistance fund for developing states.*

**Allies:** Japan, South Korea, Canada, Germany (technical)
