# Belarus — Brazil GSL POI Arsenal

## GSL only

### 1. Strongest
> **“Belarus voted against Resolution 80/57 and does not support negotiating a legally binding LAWS instrument. If Belarus accepts that autonomous weapons raise humanitarian, legal and accountability concerns, what international mechanism does it support that can actually impose common safeguards on their development and deployment?”**

**Simplified:**
> “If LAWS create serious risks, what binding international rule does Belarus support to control them?”

### 2. Existing IHL
> **“If Belarus believes existing international law is sufficient, what LAWS-specific mechanism does it support for verifying that autonomous systems remain within IHL requirements during operation?”**

**Simplified:**
> “If current law is enough, who checks that LAWS actually follow it?”

### 3. Deployment threshold
> **“Without a legally binding LAWS instrument, what common international threshold does Belarus support for deciding whether an autonomous system is sufficiently predictable and controllable to deploy?”**

**Simplified:**
> “Without a treaty, who decides when a LAWS is predictable enough?”

### 4. Accountability
> **“If Belarus opposes a new binding instrument, what additional mechanism does it support for preserving clear human responsibility when an autonomous system produces an unintended or unlawful effect?”**

**Simplified:**
> “If the weapon causes an unlawful result, what extra accountability rule does Belarus support?”

### 5. Best follow-up
> **“If Belarus's position is that existing law is sufficient, why did Belarus oppose a resolution that merely calls for completing elements for an instrument and continuing international work on LAWS?”**

**Simplified:**
> “Why oppose continued international work on LAWS if current law is already enough?”

## Factual basis
Belarus does not support negotiation of a legally binding LAWS instrument and voted against UNGA Resolution 80/57 in December 2025. It also voted against the 2024 First Committee draft resolution L.77 and Resolution 78/241 in 2023.
