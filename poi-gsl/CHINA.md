# China — Brazil GSL POI Arsenal

## GSL only

China supports negotiating a legally binding instrument but abstained on UNGA Resolution 80/57. The best approach is therefore to test the gap between support for a treaty and the conditions China wants for one.

### 1. Strongest
> **“China supports negotiating a legally binding instrument on LAWS but abstained on Resolution 80/57. What specific element of the resolution prevented China from supporting the resolution while still supporting negotiations?”**

**Simplified:**
> “China supports a treaty but abstained on the resolution. What exactly did China object to?”

### 2. Predictability
> **“If China supports binding regulation, what minimum international standard does it believe should determine whether an autonomous weapon is sufficiently predictable and controllable for deployment?”**

**Simplified:**
> “What should the international minimum be for deciding a LAWS is predictable enough?”

### 3. Human control
> **“Does China believe that human control should impose internationally defined minimum requirements on the system's target scope, duration and operating area, or should those requirements remain primarily at national discretion?”**

**Simplified:**
> “Should the basic limits on human control be international, or decided by each country?”

### 4. Deployment
> **“If China supports a legally binding instrument, does it support mandatory testing and reassessment when an autonomous system is materially modified or used outside the conditions under which it was originally assessed?”**

**Simplified:**
> “If a LAWS changes or is used in new conditions, should it have to be tested again?”

### 5. Best follow-up
> **“If China agrees that these safeguards should be binding, what prevents them from becoming explicit minimum obligations in the instrument rather than remaining matters of national implementation?”**

**Simplified:**
> “If you support the safeguards, why not make them binding minimum rules?”

## Factual basis
China supports negotiation of a legally binding LAWS instrument but abstained on UNGA Resolution 80/57 in December 2025. China has been active in CCW GGE discussions and is a useful target for testing the exact scope and binding content of its preferred framework.
