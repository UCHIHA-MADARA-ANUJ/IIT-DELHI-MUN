# Democratic People's Republic of Korea — Brazil GSL POI Arsenal

## GSL only

### 1. Strongest
> **“The Democratic People's Republic of Korea voted against Resolution 80/57. If it recognizes that autonomous weapons raise questions of humanitarian law, accountability and international security, what international framework does it support for establishing common restrictions on their development and deployment?”**

**Simplified:**
> “If LAWS create international risks, what common rules does the DPRK support to control them?”

### 2. Binding rules
> **“If the DPRK opposes the current path toward an instrument on LAWS, what alternative international mechanism does it support that would impose enforceable safeguards rather than relying on each State's national policy?”**

**Simplified:**
> “If you oppose this framework, what enforceable alternative do you propose?”

### 3. Predictability
> **“What threshold does the DPRK believe should determine whether an autonomous weapon is predictable enough to deploy, particularly when its operating environment changes after authorization?”**

**Simplified:**
> “When is a LAWS predictable enough to deploy, especially if conditions change?”

### 4. Human responsibility
> **“Does the DPRK support retaining meaningful human responsibility for the use of lethal force when an autonomous system selects or engages a target, and if so, what international rule should guarantee that responsibility?”**

**Simplified:**
> “If humans must remain responsible, what rule guarantees that?”

### 5. Best follow-up
> **“If the DPRK does not support the proposed international process, what concrete safeguard would it accept today to prevent autonomous weapons from operating without effective human control?”**

**Simplified:**
> “What safeguard would you accept right now to keep humans in control?”

## Factual basis
The DPRK was one of the states voting against UNGA Resolution 80/57 in December 2025. The POIs deliberately avoid attributing a more detailed LAWS doctrine to the DPRK than the available current record establishes; they target the consequences of its vote and force the delegate to state an alternative policy.
