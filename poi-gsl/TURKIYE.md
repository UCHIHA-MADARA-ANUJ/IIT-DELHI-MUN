# Türkiye — Brazil GSL POI Arsenal

## GSL only

### 1. Strongest
> **“Türkiye abstained on Resolution 80/57, which calls for work towards completing elements for an instrument with a view to future negotiation. Does Türkiye support moving toward a legally binding LAWS instrument, or does it believe existing law is sufficient?”**

**Simplified:**
> “Does Türkiye support a binding LAWS treaty, or are existing laws enough?”

### 2. If they support regulation generally
> **“If Türkiye supports stronger international action on LAWS, what specific obligation does it believe should be legally binding rather than left to national implementation?”**

**Simplified:**
> “What LAWS rule should actually be legally binding?”

### 3. Predictability
> **“If the acceptable level of autonomy depends on operational context, what common international threshold does Türkiye support for deciding when a system is predictable enough to deploy?”**

**Simplified:**
> “What international threshold decides when a LAWS is predictable enough?”

### 4. Human control
> **“Does Türkiye support an internationally defined minimum level of human control over critical functions of autonomous weapons, or should each State determine that threshold independently?”**

**Simplified:**
> “Should every country decide its own human-control standard?”

### 5. Best follow-up
> **“If Türkiye has not committed to a binding instrument, what mechanism does it propose to prevent States from applying fundamentally different standards to the same autonomous capability?”**

**Simplified:**
> “Without binding rules, how do we stop countries using different standards?”

## Factual basis
Türkiye has not declared support for negotiating a legally binding LAWS instrument in the current monitoring record. It abstained on UNGA Resolution 80/57 in December 2025 and also abstained on the 2024 First Committee draft resolution L.77.
